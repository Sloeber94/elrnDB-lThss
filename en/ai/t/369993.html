H20 STEERING WHEEL OSCILLATIONS/VIBRATIONS - WHILST DRIVING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The steering wheel vibrates whilst the vehicle is being driven.

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

RIM AND TYRE CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:tyre inflation pressure;that the rims are not distorted;the state of the tyresthat there is no clearance in the steering linkage

Move on to Step

1. Tyre inflation pressure not correct

1. Restore the correct pressure

2. Front rims distorted

2. Replace the distorted rimsSee in Op. 4450B10 Front WHEEL (one) - R.R.

3. Irregular tyre wear

3. Continue following the instructions in Table X50

4. Presence of clearance

4. Replace the components involved

2

CHECK ON VIBRATIONS AT LOW SPEED

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, testing the vehicle at low speed (below 60 km/h):that there are no vibrations at the steering wheel;that the vehicle does not oscillate

Move on to Step

Vibrations at the steering wheel (Shimmy): axial ovality/distortion of the front rim-tyre

Balance the front wheels < R1 > , if necessary replace the tyre/rim < R2 > See in Op. 4450B28 Left front TYRE, with wheel removed - R.R.

3

CHECK ON VIBRATIONS AT MEDIUM SPEED

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Testing the vehicle at medium speed (60-80 km/h), check whether there are vibrations at the steering wheel and the floor panel, passenger side

Move on to Step

Vibrations at the steering wheel and floor panel

Check the alignment of the driveshaft three lobe joint - gearbox. If necessary, restore the alignment

4

CHECK ON VIBRATIONS AT MEDIUM-HIGH SPEED

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Testing the vehicle at medium-high speed (above 80 km/h), check whether there are vibrations at the steering wheel

Move on to Step

1. Vibrations at the steering wheel: front wheels not balanced

1. Balance the front wheelsSee in Op. 4450B42 Front WHEELS, removed - Balancing at the bench

2. Vibrations at the steering wheel: front hubs out of alignment

2. Replace the hubsSee in Op. 4450C10 front pillar (one), l/h or r/h complete with wheel hub - r+r with wheel removed - check wheel geometry separately

3. Vibrations elsewhere: rear wheels not balanced

3. Balance the rear wheelsSee in Op. 4450B43 Rear WHEELS, removed - Balancing at the bench

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present
