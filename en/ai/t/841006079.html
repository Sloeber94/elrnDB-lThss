Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The tyre pressure control unit M047 receives a supply at pins 1e and 4 respectively along the lines protected by fuses F39 and F51; it receives an earth signal at pins 2 and 3 and is connected from pin 5, via the K line, to the EOBD diagnostic socket

See E8010 DIAGNOSTIC MULTIPLE CONNECTOR

and communicates with the Body Computer M001 via the A-BUS serial line from pin 6.

The instrument panel E050 receives a power supply at pins 1 and 2 of connector A along the lines protected by fuses F37 and F53, respectively and is connected from pins 3 and 4 connector A, via the CAN, to the Body Computer M001 and receives an earth signal at pin 7 connector A.

The Body Computer M001 receives an earth signal at pins 8 and 9 of connector E.
