Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With key on, the fuel level gauge on the instrument panel does not work (level indicated is not actual tank level);

the fuel level message is supplied by the "body computer node" and made available to the "instrument panel node" via the B-CAN network; the "Body Computer node" also sends a fuel level sensor error message.The operation should always be carried out with the engine switched off and the ignition switched off. If the operation is carried out with the engine switched on, but the ignition switched off, there may be a temporary incorrect fuel level reading due to the internal logic of the control system and not down to a system malfunction.
