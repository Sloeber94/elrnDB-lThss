1. Working at the rear of the brake pedal, press the white retaining tabs (1a) securing the brake servo push rod (1c) to the brake pedal (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB, 2.4 20V, 2.4 JTD 10V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

1. Remove the black retaining device (1a), then release the brake servo push rod (1b) from the brake pedal (1c), keep the brake servo push rod spherical heat housing cover (1d).

After every dismantling operation, the black retaining device (1a) must be replaced even if it appears to be intact.

2. Work on the fastening springs (2a) and release the clutch master cylinder push rod (2b) from the clutch pedal.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 10V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

1. Fit the new retaining device (1a) on the brake pedal (1b) so that the fastening clips (1c) are secured; position the brake servo push rod spherical head housing cover (1d).

After every dismantling operation, the black retaining device (1a) for the brake servo push rod must be replaced even if it appears to be intact.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB, 2.4 20V, 2.4 JTD 10V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

- Retract the brake servo slightly, lift up the brake pedal and position the brake servo push rod by the retaining device.

- Press the brake pedal to attach the brake servo push rod to the retaining device.

- Fasten the brake servo tightening the fixing nuts to the recommended torque.

Value - daNm
