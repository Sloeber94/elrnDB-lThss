Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the piston rings.

2. Release the pin (2a) and separate the connecting rod (2b) from the piston (2c).

Applies to: engines: 2.4 20V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Join the connecting rod to the piston so that the number stamped on the small end is facing the large references (for the inlet valves) on the piston crown.

- Fit the pin and secure it using the circlips.

Refitting

Applies to: engines: 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Join the connecting rod to the piston so that:

- on the left block, arrow (1a) printed on the piston faces the same side as printing (1b) on the connecting rod;

- on the right block, arrow (1c) printed on the piston faces the same side as printing (1d) on the connecting rod;

- Replace the pin and secure it using the circlips.
