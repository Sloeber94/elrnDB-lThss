Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

See in Op. 7055B54 PROTECTION/GUARD under engine - R.R.

- Position a suitable container for collecting the oil under the sump.

1. Undo the drain plug and collect the oil in the container.

Used engine oil contains substances that are harmful to the environment. Deposit these substances in specially designed containers which will later be disposed of.
