Refitting

- Open the boot, remove the cover on the left hand side and disconnect the negative battery terminal.

See in Op. 7040A68 GRILLE for centre dashboard speaker - R.R.

1. Undo the fixing bolts.

2. Partly remove the speaker.

- Disconnect the electrical connection and remove the speaker.
