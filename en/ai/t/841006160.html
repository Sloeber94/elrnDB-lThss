Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The controlled suspension is managed by a special electronic control unit.

The system comprises the following components:

  • three vertical acceleration sensors (2 front and one right rear) located near the bodyshell;
  • two vertical acceleration sensor located on the two front wheels;
  • one lateral acceleration sensor located at the front of the vehicle;
  • and, lastly, four variable calibration shock absorbers controlled by means of solenoid valves located internally.

The function is only enabled in the Key-on condition (INT) and if there is a speedometer signal.

The device has a special diagnostic signal

See E8010 DIAGNOSTIC MULTIPLE CONNECTOR
