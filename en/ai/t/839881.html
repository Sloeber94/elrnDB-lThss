Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

TYPE

Current developments

These engines are fitted with a high pressure electronic injection system for fast diesel engines. This 'Bosch Common Rail EDC-16C8' system is fully managed by an electronic control unit.

Specifications

5 cylinder in line, 20 valve, 2386 cc turbodiesel engine with Bosch Common Rail Unijet EDC 16C8 direct injection

POWER - 175 BHP EC (129 kW) at 4000 rpm

TORQUE - 40.10 kgm EC ( 393.4 Nm) at 2500 rpm

CYLINDER ARRANGEMENT - 5 in line

BORE - 82 mm

STROKE - 90.4 mm

CYLINDER CAPACITY - 2386 cc

CYLINDER HEAD - aluminium alloy

CYLINDER BLOCK/CRANKACSE - cast iron closed deck

CRANKSHAFT - steel with 6 main bearings

TIMING - two overhead camshafts with the inlet shaft driven directly by the timing belt transmitting power to the exhaust shaft by means of a pair of gears.

FUEL SYSTEM - unijet direct injection with variable geometry turbocharger and intercooler

IGNITION - electronic Bosch EDC 16C8

EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES - E.G.R. with catalytic converter

LUBRICATION - forced with geared pump and water/oil radiator

COOLING - liquid with forced circulation via centrifugal pump

SYSTEM DETAILS

Devices adopted

To reduce polluting emission levels, these engines are equipped with:

  • exhaust system with oxidising catalytic converter
  • Exhaust gas recirculation system (E.G.R.)
  • crankcase blowby gas recirculation system.
