Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

- Open the boot, remove the cover on the left hand side and disconnect the negative battery lead.

- Raise the vehicle.

- Position a container for collecting the radiator coolant.

1. Open the rapid attachment band (1a) and disconnect the lower pipe (1b) from the radiator (1c).

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

- Lower the vehicle.

See in Op. 7040D50 ENGINE COMPARTMENT UPPER SOUND INSULATION SHIELD - R.R.

See in Op. 1048A28 vacuum air circuit resonator pipe - r+r

See in Op. 1048A35 HOSE FROM FLOW METER TO RESONATOR - R.R.

See in Op. 1048A11 COMPLETE AIR FILTER -R.R.

See in Op. 1056B82 Injection/ignition system CONTROL UNIT (one) - R.R.

1. Remove the container positioned behind the air chamber and disconnect the two connectors (1a) for the rear Lambda sensors, the connector (1b) for the timing side detonation sensor and the coupling (1c) between the engine system cable and the vehicle wiring.

1. Working as far as possible at the rear of the engine, loosen the bolts (1a) fixing the rigid alternator coolant return pipe to the cylinder head (1b), then open the band (1c) and disconnect the rigid pipe from the hose (1d).

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

1. Open the bands (1a) retaining the rigid pipe (1b) to the hose (1c) between the pipe and the thermostat and the return pipe from the heater (1d), then remove the rigid pipe.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the pipe and close the bands securing the hoses and the heater pipe connected to it.

- Tighten the bolts fixing the rigid alternator coolant return pipe to the cylinder head.

- Connect the two connectors for the rear Lambda sensors, the connector for the timing side detonation sensor and the coupling between the engine system cable and the vehicle wiring, then position the protective container.
