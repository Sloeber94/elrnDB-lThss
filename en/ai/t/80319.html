7035A04 WINDSCREEN - Eliminate water penetration

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Wet the outer part of the windscreen glass using soapy water.

2. Blow through from the inside using an air jet and identify the penetration point.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- If the penetration area is small, detach the interfering components and seal the area involved from the inside using a suitable sealant.

- Wait for it to dry and repeat the test.

- If the penetration point is larger.

See in Op. 7035A18 Rearscreen WINDOW GLASS (bonded type) - R.R. for Replacement - Includes cleaning housing in bodyshell
