Applies to: all engines; all production periods

the heater pads and temperature selectors for the rear seats are connected to two control modules located under the seats.The temperature selectors are located in the rear seat centre armrest.The seat heating consists of two elements fitted in the seat and the backrest controlled in parallel by the control moduleThe heater pad in the seat has an NTC sensor for controlling the temperature.
