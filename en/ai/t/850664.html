- Fit the oil dipstick pipe, raise the lift and tighten the lower fixing bolt on the oil sump.

- Press fit the coupling for the engine wiring on the engine oil dipstick pipe.

- Fit the E.G.R. system solenoid valve (1a) complete with gasket and secure it tightening the bolts to the recommended torque.

- Tighten the bolts fixing the exhaust gas recirculation pipe to the EGR solenoid valve to torque.

- Connect the EGR electrical connection.
