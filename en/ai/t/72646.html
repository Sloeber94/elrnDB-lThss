L10 CLIMATE CONTROL NOT COOLING

Path

Applies to
all engines; all production periods

Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB, 2.4 20V, 3.0 V6, 2.4 JTD 10V; all production periods

The fan works normally and sends air to the passenger compartment according to the pre-set flows bu the air remains at the outside temperature and is not cooled.

The enablement of the engagement of the refrigerant circuit is controlled by both the climate control system control unit and the engine management control unit. Therefore if the climate control system is not cooling in certain conditions, this is correct.
