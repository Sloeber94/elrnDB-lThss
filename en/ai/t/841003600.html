Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The vehicle is equipped with a predisposition connection for the connection of a trailer.

This connection is located in the rear wiring and includes connections required to install a trailer power point with the tow hook to simplify installation.

The connection supplies the following signals:

  • battery positive (+30)
  • side light signal
  • turn signal signal
  • brake light signal
  • earth.
