Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When reverse gear is engaged the the warning lights do not work;

The reversing lights are controlled directly by the switch on the gearbox.The Body Computer Node receives a lights on signal from the positive supply for the reversing light bulbs

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

BULB CONDITION CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the reversing light bulbs

Move on to Step

Bulb burnt out

Replace the reversing light bulbSee in Op. 5550D26 Reversing light BULB - R.R.

2

ELECTRICAL SYSTEM CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check:the continuity/insulation between the connection in the dashboard junction unit and the reversing light bulbthe continuity/insulation between the bulb and the intermediate connectionthe continuity/insulation between the intermediate connection and the dashboard junction unit(Wiring Diagram ) E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS

Move on to Step

Wiring faulty

Restore the electrical connection(Wiring Diagram ) E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS
