Applies to: all engines; all production periods

One of the two rear fog lamps remains constantly on or off;

The rear fog lamps are controlled and diagnosed directly by the Body Computer Node.Any fault detected by the Body Computer Node is displayed on the reconfigurable matrix.

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT.

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic socket and check the following at the Body Computer Node:that there are no faults at the rear fog lamps

Move on to Step

Error at the rear fog lamps

Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

2

REAR FOG LAMP BULB CONDITION CHECK.

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the rear fog lamp bulb

Move on to Step

Bulb burnt out

Replace the rear fog lamp bulb See in Op. 5540E14 Left or right fog light BULB (one) - R.R.

3

ELECTRICAL SYSTEM CHECK.

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the continuity of the electrical connection between the rear fog lamp and the Body Computer Node (Wiring Diagram ) E2031 REAR FOGLAMPS

Move on to Step

Connection broken

Restore the electrical connection(Wiring Diagram ) E2031 REAR FOGLAMPS

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present.

Fault persists

Replace body computerSee in Op. 5505A32 BODY COMPUTER - R.R.
