1. Open the retaining band (1a) and disconnect the hose from the alternator connector.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

1. Working as far as possible at the rear of the engine, loosen the bolts (1a) fixing the rigid alternator coolant return pipe (1b) to the cylinder head in order to allow greater clearance, then open the band (1c) and remove the hose (1d).

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods
