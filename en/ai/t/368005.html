Applies to: all engines; all production periods

the demisting function for the external rear view mirrors is controlled by the Driver's Door Node and the Passenger Door Node by the command coming from the Climate Node.The information concerning the timed demisting activation is made available by the Body Computer Node for the Door Nodes on the request of the Climate Node via the B-CAN.
