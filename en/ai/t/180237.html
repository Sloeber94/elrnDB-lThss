Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Raise the vehicle.

- Using the special tool that comes in the tool kit, remove the protective wheel cover.

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the wheel assembly (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the wheel and secure it without tightening the bolts.

- Lower the vehicle.

- Tighten the bolts fixing the wheel to the recommended torque.

Value - daNm
