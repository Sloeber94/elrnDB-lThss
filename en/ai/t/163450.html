Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The tilt sensors are located on the roof node and are connected to the Body Computer Node via the A-BUS dialogue line.The tilt sensor is operated and controlled by the Body Computer Node via a series of encrypted messages and can be excluded by the customer using the special button.In the case of a failure the Body Computer Node detects and memorizes the causes of the malfunction.
