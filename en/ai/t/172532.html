5560BT MIL warning light operational check

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Make sure that the ignition key is in the OFF position.

Disconnect the connectors from the engine management node

Earth the MIL warning light control cable (see wiring diagram E5030 for petrol engines; E5050 for Diesel engines)

Turn the key to the ON position and check that the MIL warning light comes on
