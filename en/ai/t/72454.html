Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the brake pedal is pressed the travel is greater than normal

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

BRAKE PEDAL FLEXIBILITY CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the brake pedal is not fleixble.Check that the brake fluid level is correctCheck that there are no fluid leaks from: connectors, pipes, flexible pipes, reservoir, wheel cylinders, master cylinderCheck that the flexible pipes do not expand as a result of the braking actionCheck that there are no obstructions in the brake fluid reservoir opening.

Move on to Step

1. Pedal flexible

1. Bleed the airSee in Op. 3330A14 Hydraulic braking system - Bleed air

2. No fluid in the reservoir due to leaks

2. Tighten the connectors or replace the component producing the leak, then top up the levelSee in Op. 3330A10 Brake fluid - Check level and top up, if necessary

3. No fluid, but no visible leak

Move on to Step

4. Front flexible pipes expanding

4. Replace the flexible pipes

5. Opening obstructed

5. Clean the opening.

2

BRAKE PEDAL CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that there are not two pressure stages for the brake pedal and that it returns promptly

Move on to Step

1. The pedal catches: one pressure chamber is faulty

1. Replace the brake pumpSee in Op. 3330C14 MASTER CYLINDER (PUMP) - R.R. for versions with A.B.S.

2. The pedal returns with a delay: master cylinder faulty

2. Replace the brake pumpSee in Op. 3330C14 MASTER CYLINDER (PUMP) - R.R. for versions with A.B.S.

3

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic connector check the following at the Parking Brake Node (N.P.B.):in error environmentthat there are no faultsin active diagnosis ambientthe efficiency of the parking brake electric actuator by activating the brake switching on and off functions

Move on to Step

Presence of errors / actuator faulty

Continue following the instructions in the diagnostic table

4

BRAKE PEDAL FLEXIBILITY CHECK THROUGH ROAD TEST

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the brake pedal does not become too flexible after a great deal of braking with an increase in its travel (vapour lock)

Move on to Step

The pedal becomes flexible

Change the brake/clutch fluidSee in Op. 0010T62 BRAKE and/or CLUTCH FLUID Replace fluid including washing pipes and bleeding air - R.R.
