Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The rear headrestraint foldaway device allows the driver to obtain maximum rear visibility. This function is managed by the boot control unit, which is enabled by the Body Computer to download key-on status (INT).

When a foldaway command is downloaded, the control unit supplies the head restraint motors and the head restraints are stowed away inside the backrest. No specific command is used to restore the head restraints to their original position: they are simply raised by hand.
