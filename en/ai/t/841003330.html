Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The high beams are activated by the stalk switch:

  • with the low beams on, by pushing the stalk toward the facia (stable mode)
  • in flashing mode, by pulling the lever toward the steering wheel (instable mode).

The low beam activation relay is managed by the body computer: relay excitation is activated by enablement signals with key on (INT) and a specific stalk switch command.

Activation/deactivation information is sent via the CAN network: in this way, the high beam warning light is activated on the control panel.

The body computer also manages the low beam maintenance function: when the high beams are turned on, the body computer keeps the low beans on
