1. Undo the fixing bolts.

2. Disconnect the EGR valve supply pipe from the connector on the throttle casing.

3. Disconnect the vacuum pipe from the vacuum unit.

4. Undo the bolts fixing the throttle casing to the intake manifold.

5. Remove pneumatically controlled throttle casing

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the throttle casing on the intake manifold.

- Tighten the bolts fixing the throttle casing to the intake manifold.

- Connect the vacuum pipe to the connector on the vacuum unit.

- Tighten the bolts fixing the EGR valve supply pipe to the throttle casing.
