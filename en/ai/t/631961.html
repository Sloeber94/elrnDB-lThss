Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

SPECIFICATIONS

6 cylinder in 60° V, 24 valve, 2958 cc petrol engine with integrated Bosch ME 3.1 electronic injection-ignition system

POWER - 215 CV EC (158kW) at 6300 rpm

TORQUE - 26.80 kgm EC ( 263 Nm) at 5000 rpm

CYLINDER ARRANGEMENT - 6 in 60° V

BORE - 932 mm

STROKE - 72.6 mm

CYLINDER CAPACITY - 2958 cc

CYLINDER HEAD - in heated-treated aluminium

CYLINDER BLOCK/CRANKCASE - aluminium

CRANKSHAFT - in spheroidal cast iron

TIMING SYSTEM -four overhead camshafts, hydraulic tappets, 4 valves per cylinder

FUEL SYSTEM - returnless system with Bosch injection

IGNITION - Bosch integrated electronic

EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES - two catalytic converters with 4 lambda sensors

LUBICATION - forced with gear pump

COOLING - liquid with forced circulation via centrifugal pump
