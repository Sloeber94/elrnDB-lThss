Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB, 2.4 20V; all production periods

- This operation should be carried out with the engine at the bench. After having removed the power unit, separate the gearbox-differential unit from the engine and position the latter on the overhauling stand.

- Remove the front crankshaft oil seal cover following the operations illustrated in the manual for overhauling this engine at the bench.
