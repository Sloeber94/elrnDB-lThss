Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

- Open the boot and disconnect the negative battery terminal.

See in Op. 7040D50 ENGINE COMPARTMENT UPPER SOUND INSULATION SHIELD - R.R.

See in Op. 7055B54 PROTECTION/GUARD under engine - R.R.

See in Op. 4450B04 Right FRONT WHEEL (one) - R.R.

See in Op. 7055B42 FRONT WHEEL ARCH LINER (one), left or right - R.R.

See in Op. 7055B64 Engine compartment DUST SEAL in front wheel arch (one), left or right - R.R.

1. Undo the bolt fixing the reaction rod to the bodyshell.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 10V; all production periods

- Position a hydraulic lift under the gearbox to support the power unit.

1. Undo the bolts fixing the engine support to the gearbox.

- Gently lower the hydraulic lift until it is resting on the crossmember.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 10V; all production periods

1. Adjust the moving tensioner (1a) for the single drive belt using the spanner (1b), then insert the tool (1c) in the opening in the tensioner.

Tensioning auxiliary shaft belt

2. Remove the protective plug for the auxiliar shaft drive belt cam tensioner.

3. Undo the bolt retaining the cam tensioner.

4. Remove the auxiliary shaft drive belt.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 10V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the single drive belt on the pullies.

Avoid the belt coming into contact with oil or solvents that could alter the flexibility of the rubber with a resulting loss of adhesion. Also check that the belt shows no signs of cracks or cuts or else it will have to be played.

1. Rotate the cam tensioner (1a) using the tool (1b) until the clip (1c) can be released easily, then lock the bolt (1d) fixing the cam tensioner.
