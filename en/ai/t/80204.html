Removing

Refitting

1. Release the retaining spring, using a screwdriver (without removing it).

2. Detach the telescopic strut, bonnet side.

3. Undo the bolts (3a) and remove the bonnet hinge (3b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the hinge in position and screw in the bodyshell side fixing bolts, without tightening them to torque.

- Fit the end of the telescopic strut in the housing in the bonnet hinges.

See in Op. 7025A10 Bonnet Lid - Realignment
