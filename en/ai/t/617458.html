Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

WATER PUMP

Specifications

Type

This is a centrifugal vane pump with an aluminium alloy casng and phenolic resin impeller.

It is secured to the crankcase and crankshaft-driven via a poly-V belt.

THERMOSTAT

Operation

General remarks

This is fitted to the gearbox end cylinder head and maintains the engine at optimum temperature:

  • with temperature %lt; 87 ± 2°C, the thermostat valve (closed) directs fluid to the pump.
  • with temperature %lt; 87 ± 2°C, the thermostat valve (open) directs fluid to the radiator.

The engine coolant sensor fitted to the thermostat is connected to the injection control unit and control panel [DF 1056].
