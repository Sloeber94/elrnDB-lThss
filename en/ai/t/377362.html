0010T21 ENGINE COOLANT - Check density and top up if necessary

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

See in Op. 7040D50 ENGINE COMPARTMENT UPPER SOUND INSULATION SHIELD - R.R.

- Check that the level of the engine coolant is between the MIN and MAX marks on the coolant reservoir.

This check should be carried out after waiting for a few minutes with the engine switched off and the vehicle on a flat surface.

Do not remove the tank cap when the engine is hot as there is a risk of scalding.

- If necessary, top up the level, undoing the cap and introducing engine coolant.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 20V; all production periods
