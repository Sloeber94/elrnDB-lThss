1. Disconnect the pipe from the housing in the tappet cover.

2. Disconnect the pipe from the filter casing air hose to the turbocharger.

3. Remove the engine oil vapour pipe.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the oil vapour pipe on the connectors on the tappet cover and on the turbocharger air filter casing hose.

- Fasten the ends using the bands.
