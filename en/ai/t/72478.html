Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The vehicle tends to drift to the left and/or the right whilst braking.

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

0

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the diagnostic test equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the Body Computer.Check the following at the CAN:that there are no faulty control units;no control unit with faulty CAN line;

Move on to Step

Presence of errors

Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

1

OIL LEAKS CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that there are no oil leaks from:calipers;master cylinder - braking circuit

Move on to Step

1. Oil leak from one front caliper

1. Replace the faulty caliperSee in Op. 3310A30 FRONT BRAKE CALIPER (one) - R.R. - Includes bleeding air

2. Oil leak from one rear caliper

2. Replace the faulty caliperSee in Op. 3310B36 REAR BRAKE CALIPER (one), left or right - R.R.

2

BRAKE WEAR CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the brakes for wear and the condition of the braking surfaces

Move on to Step

1. Brakes very worn

1. Overhaul the brakes

2. Discs vitrified or with traces of grease

2. Clean and check the causes

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Test the vehicle before returning it to the customer, checking that the problem no longer exists.The test is essential because the braking system is one of the vehicle's safety systems.
