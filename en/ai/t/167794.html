Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The parking brake failure signal is sent from the electric Parking Brake Node (NPB) directly to the Instrument Panel Node. The Instrument Panel Node turns on a dedicated warning light and displays a parking brake fault message. The relevant message is displayed if the warning light is faulty.
