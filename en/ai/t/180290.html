- Release the A.B.S. sensor from the housing.

1. Undo the bolts fixing the hub to the stub axle.

2. Remove the rear wheel hub from the housing in the stub axle.

The bearings with multi-polar magnetic codifiers for A.B.S. active sensors should never be placed in direct contact with permanent magnets or electro-magnets.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

- Fit the rear wheel hub in the housing in the stub axle.

- Tighten the bolts fixing the hub to the stub axle to torque.
