Removing

Refitting

- Open the boot and disconnect the negative battery terminal.

1. Release the rear cover from the tunnel cover.

2. Disconnect the electrical connector for the passive entry aerial.

Description

Connector

2

RF aerial on tunnel for passive entry (NPE)

P077

3. Undo the bolts fixing the passive entry aerial.

4. Remove the aerial.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Connect the electrical connector for the passive entry aerial.

Description

Connector

.

RF aerial on tunnel for passive entry (NPE)

P077

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

- Check that the aerial is working.

- Fit the passive entry RF aerial on the trim tightening the fixing bolts.

- Fit the trim.
