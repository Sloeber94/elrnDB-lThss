1. Position a hydraulic jack under the gearbox casing and undo the two nuts (1a) fixing the gearbox side engine support (1b) to the bracket (1c), then lower the jack until the gearbox casing is resting on the suspension frame.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

1. Undo the bolt (1a) fixing the flexible mounting (1b) to the rigid support (1c).

2. Undo the upper bolt fixing the rigid support to the gearbox.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

- Raise the vehicle

1. Working via the wheel arch, undo the three side bolts fixing the rigid support to the gearbox.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

- Remove the rigid support, complete with flexible mounting, from the engine compartment.

- If necessary, replace the flexible mounting.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Proceed with reassembling the flexible mounting (1a) on the rigid support (1b) and then ensure that they are both correctly positioned in the vehicle in relation to the centering opening (1c) to allow the subsequent fastening of the support to the bodyshell.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

- Working via the wheel arch, tighten the three side bolts fixing the rigid support to the gearbox to the recommended torque.

Value - daNm
