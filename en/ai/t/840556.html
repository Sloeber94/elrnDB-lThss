FUEL HEATING DEVICE

Specifications

Type

The fuel filter is equipped with a fuel pre-heating device (1) operated by the control unit and a fuel temperature sensor (2). When the temperature of the fuel is below 6ºC, an electrical resistance heats it up to a maximum of 15 ºC before sending it to the pressure pump.
