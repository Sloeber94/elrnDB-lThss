E03 CLUTCH VIBRATING OR JERKING

all engines; all production periods

Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB, 2.4 20V, 2.4 JTD 10V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

There are vibrations and jerking at the clutch pedal. When the clutch pedal is released the vehicle does not move off smoothly, but there are vibrations or jerking.

First, check:

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

CLUTCH PEDAL CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the clutch pedal travel

Move on to Step

Pedal travel not correct

Restore the operation of the clutch pedal

2

ENGINE SUPPORT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition and centering of the front crossmember, the supports and the engine fastening connecting rod.

Move on to Step

1. Engine support fastening faulty

1. Adjust the fastenings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to the recommended torque.See in Op. 1008A10 REACTION RODS AND SUPPORTS for power unit - Check and fastening

2. Connecting rod fastening faulty

2. Adjust the fastenings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to the recommended torque.

3

CLUTCH PLATE CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check:check that the clutch plate is not worn or "vitrified"that there is no damage to the pressure plate diaphragm spring

Move on to Step

1. Clutch plate faulty

1. Replace the clutch plate < R1 > . If there is oil, replace the gearbox input oil seal < R2 > See in Op. 2110C20 SEALS FOR gearbox input shaft - Replace with gearbox removed

2. Pressure plate faulty

2. Replace the pressure plateSee in Op. 1810A20 CLUTCH - R.R.

4

SPLINED HUB CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the clutch plate hub, the gearbox main shaft and the condition of the assembly

Move on to Step

1. Clutch plate hub faulty

1. Replace the clutch plateSee in Op. 1810A20 CLUTCH - R.R.

2. Gearbox main shaft faulty

2. Replace the gearbox main shaftSee in Op. 2110B20 MANUAL GEARBOX (5 speed) and differential - Dismantling and reassembly - Washing components - Replacing synchronizers and internal controls, if necessary

5

FLYWHEEL CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the flywheel (in particular the condition of the surface and make sure that there is no overheating, uneven wear, incisions or material removed) and the operation of the flexible couplings

Move on to Step

Flywheel damaged

Replace the flywheelSee in Op. 1024B22 ENGINE FLYWHEEL - R.R. with clutch removed for versions with manual gearbox

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present
