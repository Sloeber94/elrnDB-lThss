Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the key is turned to the ON position nothing happens, the instruments do not light up; In the starting position, the starter motor does not turn.

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

BATTERY EFFICIENCY CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the efficiency of the battery using the appropriate diagnostic equipment

Move on to Step

Battery not working properly

Replace the battery

2

FASTENINGS CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:Correct fastening and earth connection of the negative battery terminalCorrect fastening of positive battery pole and all leads connected to itNo oxidation of the lead fasteningsCorrect fastening and connection between positive pole and junction with maxifuse at the battery

Move on to Step

1. Lead fastenings at the battery slack

1. Tighten leads

2. Earth lead frayed

2. Replace leadSee in Op. 5530B22 battery earth lead - r+r

3. Connections with slack fastenings

3. Restore fastenings

4. Maxifuse burnt out

4. Check the causes of the blown fuse and replace it(Wiring Diagram ) E1010 POWER SUPPLY

3

SUPPLY CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check switch power supply

Move on to Step

The power supply does not reach the switch

Check the continuity of the wiring between the Maxifuse in the battery junction unit and the switch(Wiring Diagram ) E1010 POWER SUPPLY

4

SWITCH CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the switch and that it is working properly

Move on to Step

Switch contacts not working properly

Replace the switchSee in Op. 5520A12 IGNITION SWITCH ASSEMBLY - R.R. for versions with AIR BAG

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present
