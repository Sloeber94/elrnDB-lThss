Removing

Refitting

- Open the boot and disconnect the negative battery terminal.

- Position a suitable container for collecting the headlamp washer fluid.

- Disconnect the pipe from the headlamp washer fluid reservoir to the jet, jet side.

1. Undo the bolts fixing the jet to the vehicle.

2. Remove the jet.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the jet tightening the fixing bolts.

- Connect the pipe from the headlamp washer fluid reservoir to the jet, jet side.

- Top up the headlamp washer fluid level in the reservoir.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.
