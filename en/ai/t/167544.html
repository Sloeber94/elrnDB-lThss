Applies to: all engines; all production periods

One of the two dipped headlamps is not working;

The dipped headlamps are controlled by the Body Computer Node via a relay located in the Dashboard Junction Unit.

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

ELECTRICAL SYSTEM CHECK.

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the fuse for the light power circuit in the Dashboard Junction Unit(Wiring Diagram ) E2011 DIPPED BEAMS

Move on to Step

Fuse blown

Replace the fuse(Wiring Diagram ) E2011 DIPPED BEAMS

2

XENON BULB CONDITION CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Extract the light from its housing dismantling it and checking the condition of the xenon bulb

Move on to Step

Bulb burnt out

Replace the bulb

3

LAMP ELECTRICAL SYSTEM CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the cables inside the lamp between the connector and the module for the xenon bulb.

Move on to Step

interruption or no insulation for the internal cables.

Restore the electrical connection, if necessary, replace the lamp(Wiring Diagram ) E2011 DIPPED BEAMS

4

ELECTRICAL SYSTEM CHECK.

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the continuity and the insulation of the wiring between the headlamps and the earth(Wiring Diagram ) E2011 DIPPED BEAMS

Move on to Step

Interruption or no insulation for the electrical connection

Restore the electrical connection(Wiring Diagram ) E2011 DIPPED BEAMS

5

ELECTRICAL SYSTEM CHECK.

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the continuity and the insulation of the wiring between the front headlamp and the Dashboard Junction Unit(Wiring Diagram ) E2011 DIPPED BEAMS

Move on to Step

Interruption or no insulation for the electrical connection

Restore the electrical connection(Wiring Diagram ) E2011 DIPPED BEAMS

6

ELECTRICAL SYSTEM CHECK.

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the continuity and the insulation of the internal wiring between the outlet connector for the Dashboard Junction Unit and the Fuse for the power circuit(Wiring Diagram ) E2011 DIPPED BEAMS

Move on to Step

Interruption or no insulation for the Dashboard Junction Unit internal connection

Replace Dashboard Junction UnitSee in Op. 5505A10 Junction UNIT - R.R.

7

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists.

Replace xenon bulb electronic module
