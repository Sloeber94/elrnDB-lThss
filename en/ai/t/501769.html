0010T11 ENGINE OIL - Check level and top up if necessary

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Release the dipstick.

This check should be carried out after waiting for a few minutes with the engine switched off and the vehicle on a flat surface.

- Using the dipstick, check that the oil is between the MIN and MAX levels on the actual dipstick.

- The interval between MIN and MAX corresponds to about 1 kg fluid.

- If necessary, top up the fluid by undoing the cap and adding engine oil.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB, 2.4 20V; all production periods

- Start up the vehicle and let the engine idle for a few minutes.

- Switch off the engine and wait for a few minutes.

- Check the level of the oil once again using the dipstick.
