Applies to: all engines; all production periods

the external rear view mirrors are controlled directly by the Door Nodes according to information received by the joystick selector on the left front door handle.The mirror folding information for the Driver's Door Node is direct, the information for the Passenger Door Node is made available by the Driver's Door Noed via the B-CAN.
