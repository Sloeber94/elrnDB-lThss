0010T40 MANUAL GEARBOX OIL - Change

Path
Procedures > 00 MAINTENANCE > 0010 NEW UNITS AND MAINTENANCE > 0010T vehicle oil and fluids > 0010T40 MANUAL GEARBOX OIL - Change
Kind
repair procedure (removal / refitting; the operation number is the code)
Applies to
engines: 2.0 TB, 2.4 20V, 2.4 JTD 10V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods
Interactive page
https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB-lThss/en/#/74480

Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB, 2.4 20V; all production periods

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

- Raise the vehicle.

See in Op. 7055B54 PROTECTION/GUARD under engine - R.R.

1. Undo the gearbox casing drain plug and collect the fluid in a suitable container.

Do not let the oil disperse into the atmosphere because this could cause pollution.

- Clean the drain plug.

- Screw in the drain plug and tighten it to the recommended torque.

2. Remove the fluid dipstick and add fluid to the gearbox through the filler hole.
