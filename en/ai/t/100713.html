Removing

Refitting

- Open the boot and disconnect the negative battery terminal.

1. Disconnect the electrical connection for the fuel temperature sensor.

2. Undo the fuel temperature sensor and remove it.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the fuel temperature sensor.

- Connect the electrical connection to the fuel temperature sensor.

- Connect the negative battery lead and close the boot.
