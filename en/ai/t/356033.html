Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The flow of air sent by the fan in the passenger compartment using the manual speed control is not sufficient; it does not correspond to the speed set.

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

With the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) connected, check the following at the climate control node: that there are no faults;

Move on to Step

Presence of errors

Continue following the instructions in the diagnostic table

2

POLLEN FILTER CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the pollen filterIt should be remembered that the pollen filter should be replaced once a year

Move on to Step

Filter obstructed

Replace the pollen filterSee in Op. 5010B08 Climate control system outside air POLLEN FILTER - R.R.

3

AIR DUCTS CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the air ducts and the vents are intact and that there are no obstuctions

Move on to Step

Ducts or vents obstructed

Restore the operation of the ducts and vents

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Replace the climate control system control unitSee in Op. 5040D18 Air conditioning CONTROL UNIT - R.R.
