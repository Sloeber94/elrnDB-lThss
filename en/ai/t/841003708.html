Operational Description

According to the battery voltage control logic described previously, the Body Computer M001 supplies the driver's and passenger sun visor lights and the glove compartment light from pins 23 and 33 of connector G and pin 16 of connector D, respectively.

Once the boot release switch I011 is pressed, when the actual boot is opened, the switch on the outer handle F055 sends an earth signal to the luggage compartment control unit M063 at pin 26 of connector F which, via a further negative signal from pin 1 of connector D, controls the activation of the luggage compartment light G040, with a +30 battery supply via fuse F032 of the junction unti under the dashboard B002.
