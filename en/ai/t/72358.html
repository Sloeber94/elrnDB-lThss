It is difficult to engage the gears in all circumstances.

GEARBOX CONTROL CABLES FASTENING CHECK

Check that the fastenings for the gearbox control cables are not slack or incorrectly secured

Fasten the gearbox control cables correctly

Check the condition of the flexible gearbox control cables and make sure they slide correctly
