SPECIFICATIONS

The Luggage Compartment Node is an electronic control unit that integrates several functions. It is located inside the luggage compartment and is fastened to the frame of the left wheel arch.

OPERATION

The Luggage Compartment Node carries out the following functions:

  • FIS status signal anagement [F005]
  • Network key status (INT) [F040]
  • Dimmed lighting [F041]
  • Seat heating [F065]
  • Supply for adjustable steering column and electric seats [F066]
  • Up/down movement of rearscreen blind [F070]
  • Timed lighting of interior handles [F074]
  • Courtesy lights management [F089]
  • Management of doors/boot and fuel flap locking/unlocking [F092]
  • Unlocking of doors by inertia (F.I.S.) [F095]
  • Unlocking/opening of boot [F099]
  • Management of rear electric windows [F105]
  • Inhibition from driver's seat of rear window movement [F107]
  • Rearscreen intermittent wiper and rearscreen washer with intelligent cycle [F147]
  • Automatic activation of rearscreen wiper with reverser gear [F149]
  • Timed lighting in luggage compartment [F225]
  • Folding of rear head restraints [F226]
  • Electric child-proof locks [F263]
  • Electronic safety knob [F264]
  • Electric door/boot opening [F301]
  • Dead-lock engagement/release [F312]
  • Vehicle protection system signals [F322]

In addition it:

  • receives and transmits information on the B-CAN line (e.g.: diagnosis, warnings, commands, data)
  • connects with the rear wiring
