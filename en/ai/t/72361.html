It is difficult to engage the gears; there is also a (grating) noise coming from the area of the gearbox

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

CLUTCH CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

With the engine running and the vehicle stationary check that there is no (grating) noise when all the gears are engaged.

Move on to Step

1. The gearbox grates in all gears

1. Continue following the instructions in TABLE E001Continue following the instructions in the table E01 CLUTCH DOES NOT RELEASE CORRECTLY

2. The gearbox does not grate in all gears (it only grates in some gears)

Move on to Step

2

GEARBOX CONDITION CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the gearbox internal components

Move on to Step

1. Pre-synchronizers damaged

1. Overhaul gearboxSee in Op. 2110B20 MANUAL GEARBOX (5 speed) and differential - Dismantling and reassembly - Washing components - Replacing synchronizers and internal controls, if necessary

2. Synchronizers damaged or worn

2. Overhaul gearboxSee in Op. 2110B20 MANUAL GEARBOX (5 speed) and differential - Dismantling and reassembly - Washing components - Replacing synchronizers and internal controls, if necessary

3. Gears worn

3. Overhaul gearboxSee in Op. 2110B20 MANUAL GEARBOX (5 speed) and differential - Dismantling and reassembly - Washing components - Replacing synchronizers and internal controls, if necessary
