Applies to: engines: 2.4 20V; all production periods

WATER PUMP

Specifications

Type

This is centrifugal, vane type with an aluminium alloy pump casing and phenolic resin impeller.

Secured to the engine crankcase and driven directly by timing belt.

THERMOSTAT

Operation

General remarks

Fitted to the rear of the cylinder head and performs the function of maintaining the engine at optimum temperature:

  • with temperature < 83 +/- 2°C the thermostat valve (closed) deviates fluid directly toward the pump.
  • with temperature > 83 +/- 2°C the thermostat valve (open) deviates coolant directly toward the radiator.

The engine coolant temperature sensor fitted to the thermostat is connected to the injection control unit and control panel [DF 1056B].
