Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Operational Description

The earths present on the vehicle, categorised according to their relationship with the various wires, are as follows.

  • battery earth C001.

Front wiring:

  • left front earth C010;
  • right front earth C010;
  • ABS front earth C012.
  • engine earth C040 (for 2.4 JTD 10V, 2.4 JTD 20V, 2.4 JTD 20V CAE versions)

Facia wiring:

  • central facia earth C022;
  • Airbag earth C050.

Rear wiring:

  • left rear earth C030;
  • right rear earth C030;
  • amplifier earth C050;
  • electric brake earth C057
  • radio diversity earth C058
  • TV diversity earth C059
  • C015 driver's dashboard earth
  • passenger side dashboard earth C020
  • Side Bag system earth C055
  • passive entry aerial earth C094

Engine wiring:

  • engine earth C040; (for 2.4 petrol, 3.0 V6, 2.0 TB versions)
  • injection control unit earth C060.

TV braid wiring:

  • TV tuner earth C070.

Air conditioning wiring:

  • air conditioning unit earth C016

Rearscreen negative wiring:

  • rearscreen earth C032
