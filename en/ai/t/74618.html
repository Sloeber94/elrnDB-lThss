Refitting

- Open the boot and disconnect the negative battery terminal.

1. Undo the bolt fixing the reaction rod to the mounting bracket on the bodyshell.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 20V; all production periods

- Position the hydraulic lift under the engine oil sump in order to work safely.

1. Undo the nut (1a) fixing the rigid rear support (1b) to the mounting.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 20V; all production periods

1. Undo the nut (1a) fixing the compressor/engine support (1b) to the flexible mounting.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 20V; all production periods

1. Undo the retaining bolts.

- Using the hydraulic lift, slightly raise the engine until the support slips away from the flexible mounting stud.

2. Remove the flexible mounting from the housing in the crossmember.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 20V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Working with the hydraulic lift positioned under the engine oil sump, place the flexible mounting on the crossmember.

- Tighten the bolts fixing the flexible mounting to the engine supporting crossmember to the recommended torque.

Value - daNm

- Lower the vehicle.

1. Remove the staking from the nut fixing the front wheel hub to the driveshaft and undo the nut.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

- Position a hydraulic jack under the engine sump.

1. Undo the nut (1a) fixing the timing side front support (1b) to the flexible mounting (1b) and the two bolts (1c) fixing the flexible mounting to the chassis.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

- Raise the jack, as much as necessary, in order to be able to remove the flexible mounting from the engine supporting frame.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Correctly position the flexible mounting in its housing in the engine supporting frame.

- Tighten the two bolts fixing the flexible mounting to the engine supporting frame to the recommended torque.
