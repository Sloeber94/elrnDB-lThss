Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB, 2.4 20V, 3.0 V6, 2.4 JTD 10V; all production periods

The engine oil pressure fault message is displayed at the matrix;

the engine oil pressure fault and engine oil pressure sensor fault messages are made available by the Engine Management Node at the C-CAN and via the Body Computer Node gateway to the B-CAN and consequently to the instrument panel node.
