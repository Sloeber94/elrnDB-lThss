Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

Move the brake caliper pistons away in order to release the brake pads from the rear brake disc using the EXAMINER diagnostic equipment.

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

See in Op. 4450B11 Rear WHEEL (one) - R.R.

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the brake caliper assembly (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Remove the rear brake pads.

2. Undo the fixing bolts (2a) and remove the brake caliper bracket (2b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the fixing pin (1a) and remove the brake disc (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

- Fit the brake disc in the housing on the hub.

- Tighten the pin fixing the brake disc to torque.

- Fit the rear brake pads.

- Fit the brake caliper assembly on the hub.

- Tighten the bolts fixing the bracket to the wheel hub.

- Fit the rear brake caliper and tighten the fixing bolts.

- Tighten the bolts fixing the brake caliper to the bracket to torque.
