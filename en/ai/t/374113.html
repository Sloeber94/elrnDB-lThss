the driver's seat heater pad and potentiometer are connected to the Driving Position Node.The seat heater pad consists of two elements, fitted in the seat and the backrest, controlled in parallel by the Driving Position Node.The heater pad in the seat has an NTC sensor for controlling the temperature.
