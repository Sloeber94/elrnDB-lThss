Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 10V; all production periods

STRUCTURE

This takes the form of a pedal conneced via a special link to the accelerator pedal potentiometer [DF 1060G]. This potentiometer informs the injection/ignition control unit of accelerator pedal position.

This is fitted to the vehicle floorpan with 3 bolts and incorporates a potentiometer
