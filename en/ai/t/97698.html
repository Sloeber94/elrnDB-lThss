Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Remove the engine oil filler plug.

- Undo the bolts fixing the sound-proofing cover.

- Remove the sound-proofing cover from its housing in the engine.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the sound-proofing cover in its housing on the cylinder head.

- Tighten the bolts fixing the sound-proofing cover.

- Tighten the engine oil filler plug.
