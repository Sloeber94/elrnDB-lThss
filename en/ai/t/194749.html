7045B12 Front seat head restraint - R.R. with seat removed

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Raise the head restraint until it is in the end of travel position.

- Release the head restraint from the housing and remove it.

When carrying out the operation of removing the head restraint, a certain amount of force must be exerted from the bottom upwards, acting on the lower edges of the actual head restraint.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Fit the head restraint, inserting the pins in the bushes and positioning them on the backrest.
