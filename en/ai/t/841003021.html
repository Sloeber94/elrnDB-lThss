Refitting

Op. 7045A98 FRONT SEAT REAR POUCH PANEL, LH OR RH - R R

1. Disconnect the circlip.

2. Move the backrest pump away.

3. Disconnect the electrical connection for the backrest fan.

4. Disconnect the side bag handle.

5. Release the backrest flexible mounting support hooks.

6. Remove the front seat backrest flexible mounting complete with fan.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the front seat backrest flexible mounting complete with fan.

Secure the backrest flexible mounting support hooks.

Connect the side bag handle.

Connect the electrical connection for the backrest fan.

Place the backrest pump in position.

Connect the circlip.
