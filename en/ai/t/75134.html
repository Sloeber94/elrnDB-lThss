1076B34 Front catalytic converter attachment SEAL - Replace

Applies to: engines: 2.4 20V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

See in Op. 7055B54 PROTECTION/GUARD under engine - R.R.

1. Undo the nuts fixing the catalytic converter to the centre section of the exhaust pipe.

2. Release the pins (2a) from the pipe rubber mountings (2b).

3. Remove the seal from its housing.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 20V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the seal in the special housing in the catalyzer.

- Secure the pins to the rubber mountings.

- Tighten the nuts fixing the catalytic converter to the centre section of the exhaust pipe.

Value - daNm

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

1. Undo the nuts (1a) fixing the pipes (1b) between the catalytic converter (1c) and the preconverters (1d).

2. Remove the seals.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

Removing

- Position the seals.

- Tighten the nuts fixing the pipes between the catalytic converter and the preconverters.
