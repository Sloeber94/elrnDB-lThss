7045A98 Front seat rear pouch panel, left or right - R.R.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

See in Op. 7045A10 FRONT SEAT (one) left or right - R.R.

See in Op. 7045J10 switch unit for movement of one front seat, l/h or r/h - r+r for check

See in Op. 7045A42 Front seat GUARD (one), left or right - R.R.

- < R1 > [all the elements]

1. Open the lower part of the panel (1a) on both sides in the direction shown by the arrow and release the internal retaining spring from the housing (1b) in the backrest.

2. Undo the lower fixing bolts.

3. Slightly lower the retaining springs (3a) from the housings until they are released and remove the rear pocket panel (3b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the rear pocket panel in the housing, checking that the retaining springs are correctly inserted in the housings.

- Tighten the lower fixing bolt.

See in Op. 7045A42 Front seat GUARD (one), left or right - R.R.

See in Op. 7045J10 switch unit for movement of one front seat, l/h or r/h - r+r for check

See in Op. 7045A10 FRONT SEAT (one) left or right - R.R.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.
