Removing

Refitting

- Unscrew the exhaust timing control pulley bolt loosened previously.

- Remove the exhaust side timing drive pulley.

- Prise up and remove the exhaust side camshaft front oil seal.

Take care not to damage the seat of the oil seal during its removal. The oil seal removal operation is destructive and the seal must therefore always be replaced.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods
