Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The climate control recirculation does not work in any conditions (manual/automatic)

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket.Check that there are no faults in the climate control system control unit

Move on to Step

1. Error detected in the control unit

1. Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

2. No communication with the control unit

2. Restore connection between the climate control unit and diagnostic connector(Wiring Diagram ) E6020 AIR CONDITIONING

2

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) and carry out the active diagnosis for the recirculation motor

Move on to Step

Recirculation motor faulty

Move on to Step

3

MOTOR ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the recirculation motor electrical circuit (Wiring Diagram ) E6020 AIR CONDITIONING

Move on to Step

1. Recirculation motor connection faulty

1. Restore the correct connection(Wiring Diagram ) E6020 AIR CONDITIONING

2. Connections correct, flap/motor unit faulty

2. Check that the flap moves freely, if necessary, replace the recirculation motorSee in Op. 5040D88 MOTOR for ANY COMPARTMENT - R.R. with air conditioning casing removed

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Replace the climate control system control unitSee in Op. 5040D18 Air conditioning CONTROL UNIT - R.R.
