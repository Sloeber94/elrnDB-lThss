Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The alignment of the headlamps is not corrected as the angle of the vehicle changes;

The alignment of the headlamps is corrected automatically and continuously by a Headlamp Alignment Corrector (C.A.F.) control unit.The correction of the headlamp alignment is activated by switching on the dipped headlamps; the C.A.F. control unit has an autodiagnostic function and, if the various components are faulty, the warning light (matrix message) in the Instrument Panel Node comes on.
