7035B26 REARSCREEN GLASS - Clean edges and sand small abrasions

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The remaining sealant does not have to be removed completely. Take care not to damage the black serigraphy at the perimeter of the glass. If the centering pins break after the removal operation, the rearscreen can be reused.

1. Using suitable equipment (1a) cut and level the bead of sealant (1b) on the rearscreen so that it is as thin as possible (about 1 mm).

2. Clean the perimeter of the serigraphed area of the windscreen thoroughly with compressed air and degrease it using heptane.

- When bonding, 60 minutes after the bead of sealant has been cut and levelled, the surface of the actual sealant must be treated using the special activating product and it is necessary to wait for 15 minutes before fitting the rearscreen.
