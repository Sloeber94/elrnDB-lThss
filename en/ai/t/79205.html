- Position a suitable container for collecting the coolant.

- Open the retaining bands and disconnect the heater supply and return pipes, heater side.

1. Undo the fixing nuts (1a) and remove the heater rigid pipes mounting bracket (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the fixing bolts (1a) and remove the upper bracket (1b) and the lower bracket (1c) on the fan assembly.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts (1a) fixing the heater unit to the dashboard supporting crossmember (1b), fan assembly side.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the upper (1a) and lower (1b) fixing bolts and remove the windscreen air vent (1c) releasing it from the electrical wiring for the solar sensor.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Release the electrical wiring from the retaining springs, fastened to the air conditioning unit.

1. Undo the fixing bolts (1a) and detach the earth node (1b) and the earth lead (1c) fastened to the bodyshell, centre console side.
