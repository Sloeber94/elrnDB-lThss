Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the key is turned to the ON position, the instruments light up; in the Starting position, the starter motor does not turn.

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

MOTOR POWER SUPPLY CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the supply lead between the battery and the starter motor is intact and securely fastened

Move on to Step

1. Lead fastening slack

1. Secure the lead

2. Lead broken

2. Replace the lead

2

STARTER MOTOR RELAY FEED CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the connection between the switch and the starter motor relay and the operation of the relay

Move on to Step

1. No continuity between the switch and the engine compartment junction unit

1. Restore wiring

2. No continuity in the engine compartment junction unit

2. Replace the engine compartment junction unitSee in Op. 5550A13

3. No continuity between the engine compartment junction unit and the starter motor relay

3. Restore wiring

4. Relay damaged

4. Replace the relaySee in Op. 5520B18 Starter motor SOLENOID - R.R. with motor removed

3

SWITCH CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the operation and the condition of the switch. Operate the switch and check the contact

Move on to Step

No contact at the pin for the starter motor relay

Replace the ignition switchSee in Op. 5520A12 IGNITION SWITCH ASSEMBLY - R.R. for versions with AIR BAG

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Replace the starter motorSee in Op. 5520B12 STARTER MOTOR - R.R. for versions with air conditioning
