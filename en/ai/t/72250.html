Applies to: all engines; all production periods

See in Op. 7040A26 Steering column TRIM - R.R.

Press the Cruise Control RECALL button and check the electrical continuity between pins 2 and 1 (see wiring diagram E5060)

See in Op. 5550A12 complete stalk unit - r+r for versions with air bag

If there is no continuity, replace the steering column switch unit complete with Cruise Control system control
