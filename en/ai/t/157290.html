Refitting

- Open the boot and disconnect the negative battery terminal.

1. Release the rear climate control unit panel (1a) from the housing in the rear passenger air vent (1b) in the tunnel cover.

working carefully taking care not to damage the tunnel cover

2. Disconnect the electrical connector.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

- Connect the electrical connector for the climate control rear panel.

- Position the climate control rear panel in the housing in the air vent.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.
