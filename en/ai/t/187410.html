Removing

Refitting

1. Loosen the bands securing the intake manifolds to the air chamber.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and rmeove the intake manifolds (2b).

3. Remove the seals.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the intake manifolds complete with new seals and secure them using the bolts.

- Tighten the bands securing the intake manifolds to the air chamber.
