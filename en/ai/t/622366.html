Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB; all production periods

STRUCTURE

It comprises a pedal connected by means of a special rod to the accelerator pedal potentiometer [DF 1056]. The latter informs the injection/ignition control unit of the position of the accelerator pedal.

It is fitted on the floor of the vehicle fastened by 3 bolts and incorporates a potentiometer.
