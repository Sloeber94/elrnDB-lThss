Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB; all production periods

TYPE

Current developments

This engine is fitted with a Bosch Motronic electronic ignition system fully managed by an electronic control unit.

Specifications

5 cylinder in-line engine, single-point injection, 20 valves, 1998 cc., turbo petrol, with integrated Bosch ME 7.3.1 electronic injection-ignition system

POWER OUTPUT - 185 BHP EC (136 kW) at 5500 rpm

TORQUE - 31.40 kgm EC ( 308 Nm) at 2200 rpm

CYLINDER ARRANGEMENT - 5 in line

BORE - 82 mm

STROKE - 75.65 mm

CYLINDER CAPACITY - 1998 cc

CYLINDER HEAD - in heated-treated aluminium

CRANKCASE - in laminar cast iron

CRANKSHAFT - in spheroidal cast iron

TIMING SYSTEM - two overhead camshafts, hydraulic tappets, 4 valves per cylinder

FUEL SYSTEM - returnless system with Bosch injection

IGNITION - Bosch integrated electronic

EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES - catalytic converter and two lambda sensors

LUBICATION - forced with gear pump

COOLING - liquid with forced circulation via centrifugal pump

SYSTEM DETAILS

Devices adopted

To reduce polluting emission levels, these engines are equipped with:

  • exhaust system with catalytic converter and lambda sensors;
  • fuel vapour recirculation.
  • crankcase blowby gas recirculation system.
