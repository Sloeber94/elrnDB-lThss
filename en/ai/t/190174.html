- Position a suitable container for collecting the engine coolant.

1. Open the band (1a) for the heat exchanger (1c) flexible coolant outlet pipe (1b) and drain the fluid.

2. Open the band (2a) retaining the flexible pipe to the rigid pipe (2b) and remove the flexible pipe.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

- Open the boot, remove the cover on the left hand side and disconnect the negative battery lead.

See in Op. 1048A10 COMPLETE AIR FILTER -R.R.

- Raise the vehicle on the lift.

See in Op. 7055B54 PROTECTION/GUARD under engine - R.R.

- Position a suitable container for collecting the engine coolant.

1. Open the band (1a) on the automatic transmission fluid heat exchange and the bands (1c) on the cylinder block/crankcase coolant return pipe connector.

2. Remove the cylinder block/crankcase water supply pipe from the automatic trnasmission fluid heat exchanger draining the coolant into the container positioned previously.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 20V; all production periods

Removing

- Connect the cylinder block/crankcase water return pipe to the automatic transmission heat exchanger using the band and fasten it.

- Connect the connector to the cylinder block/crankcase coolant return pipe, then close the retaining bands.
