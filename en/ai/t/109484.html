Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

Before proceed with the operation of removing the solenoid unit, position the gear lever in "N"

- Open the boot, remove the cover on the left hand side and disconnect the negative battery lead.

See in Op. 7040D50 ENGINE COMPARTMENT UPPER SOUND INSULATION SHIELD - R.R.

See in Op. 1048A21 AIR INLET HOSE FROM INTAKE TO FILTER - R.R.

See in Op. 1048A28 vacuum air circuit resonator pipe - r+r

See in Op. 1016A42 LEFT CYLINDER HEAD IGNITION COIL COVER - R.R.

See in Op. 1048A11 COMPLETE AIR FILTER -R.R.

- Raise the vehicle on the lift.

See in Op. 7055B54 PROTECTION/GUARD under engine - R.R.

- Position a container to collect the automatic transmission fluid.

1. Undo the plug and drain the transmission fluid

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

1. Undo the lower bolts (1a) fixing the solenoid valve unit cover (1b).

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

- Lower the vehicle

1. Release the air conditioning system pipe (1b) from the retaining spring (1a)

2. Undo the upper bolts (2a) fixing the solenoid valve unit cover (2b) and remove it taking care not to damage the gasket (2c) underneath.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

1. Disconnect the eight connectors (1a) for the solenoid valves.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

1. Undo the bolt (1a) fixing the retaining bracket and remove the oil temperature sensor (1b).

proceed carefully in order not to damage the electrical connection

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

1. Undo the two bolts (1a) fixing the inlet cover (1b) and remove it together with the gasket.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts fixing the solenoid valve unit starting with the top ones (1a) and then, after having raised the vehicle, finish with the bottom ones (1b).

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

1. Disconnect the gear selector lever (1a) and remove the solenoid valve unit (1b) releasing it from underneath.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

1. Remove the two gaskets underneath the solenoid valve unit

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods
