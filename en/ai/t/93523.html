Removing

Refitting

- Make sure that the ignition key is in the OFF position, then disconnect the negative battery lead.

See in Op. 1016A40 Ignition COIL COVER on cylinder head - R.R.

1. Disconnect the electrical connection from the phase transformer solenoid.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the phase transformer solenoid (2b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB, 2.4 20V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the phase transformer solenoid in its housing and secure it using the bolts.

- Connect the electrical connection to the phase transformer solenoid.

See in Op. 1016A40 Ignition COIL COVER on cylinder head - R.R.

- Connect the negative battery lead.
