1. Disconnect the electrical connection from the air flow meter.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB, 2.4 20V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Thoroughly clean the air flow meter and the areas connected with the air filter casing in order to prevent any dirty air from passing through.

- Place the air flow meter in its housing and secure it using the plates and the nuts.

- Connect the electrical connection to the air flow meter.
