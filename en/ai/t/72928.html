X01 EXCESSIVE FUEL CONSUMPTION

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 10V; all production periods

The engine seems to be running smoothly, but the fuel consumption is higher than ever before.

The fuel consumption figures in the technical documentation have been defined during the couse of homologation tests in accordance with directive 93/116/CE.These tests simulate an urban cycle and an average non-urban cycle and therefore may not be representative as fuel consumption depends a great deal on driving styles.
