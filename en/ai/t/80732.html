7055B54 PROTECTION/GUARD under engine - R.R.

Path
Procedures > 70 FITTINGS > 7055 MOULDINGS ON PROTECTIONS > 7055B EXTERIOR PROTECTION > 7055B54 PROTECTION/GUARD under engine - R.R.
Kind
repair procedure (removal / refitting; the operation number is the code)
Applies to
all engines; all production periods
Interactive page
https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB-lThss/en/#/80732

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

- Raise the vehicle.

1. Undo the fixing bolts (1a) for the rear protection under the engine (1b) and remove it.

2. Undo the fixing bolts (2a) for the front protection under the engine (2b) and remove it.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the front engine flywheel guard and tighten the retaining bolts.

- Fit the rear engine flywheel guard and tighten the retaining bolts.

- Lower the vehicle and remove it from the lift.
