Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB, 2.4 20V, 2.4 JTD 10V; all production periods

When the clutch pedal is pressed, the resistance is excessive

Since this symptom is "subjective", make sure that there is actually some hardening compared with what was previously considered as "normal"

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

CLUTCH PEDAL CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the clutch pedal travel and operation

Move on to Step

Pedal/pedal assembly faulty

Restore the operation of the clutch pedal

2

CLUTCH PUMP AND PIPE CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the operation of the pump, the impulse damper and make sure that there are no obstructions in the clutch pipes

Move on to Step

Clutch actuator oil leaks or faulty

Replace the components involvedSee in Op. 1820B48 FLEXIBLE SUPPLY PIPE to operating cylinder - R.R.

3

CLUTCH CONTROL ACTUATOR CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the clutch actuator, inside the bellhousing

Move on to Step

Clutch actuator oil leaks or faulty

Replace the components involvedSee in Op. 1820B48 FLEXIBLE SUPPLY PIPE to operating cylinder - R.R.

4

SPLINED HUB CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for grease on the clutch plate hub bushes and the gearbox main shaft and check the condition of the assembly

Move on to Step

1. Clutch plate hub faulty

1. Restore the correct lubrication of the bushes and, if necessary, replace the clutch plateSee in Op. 1810A20 CLUTCH - R.R.

2. Gearbox main shaft hub faulty

2. Replace the gearbox main shaftSee in Op. 2110B20 MANUAL GEARBOX (5 speed) and differential - Dismantling and reassembly - Washing components - Replacing synchronizers and internal controls, if necessary

5

Move on to Step

Clutch plate or pressure plate faulty

Replace the clutch plateSee in Op. 1810A20 CLUTCH - R.R.

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present
