Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

See in Op. 7055B54 PROTECTION/GUARD under engine - R.R.

1. Disconnect the engine oil vapour pipe from the separator.

2. Disconnect the engine oil vapour pipe from the oil sump.

3. Remove the engine oil recovery pipe frm the lower part of the engine compartment.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the engine oil recovery pipe and connect to the engine oil sump.

- Connect the engine oil recovery pipe to the engine oil vapour separator.

See in Op. 7055B54 PROTECTION/GUARD under engine - R.R.

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.
