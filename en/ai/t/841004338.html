Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The front ashtray is lit with the side lights on. It houses a standard power point surrounded by a ring that is also lit with the side lights on. The socket contains a cigar lighter heater coil that can also be used to connect other instruments or devices because it works it is a 12V power point.

Press the cigar lighter knob to operate the lighter: after a few seconds it will emerge automatically, ready for use.

The socket is equipped with a thermal protection device: this protection cuts off the power supply if devices that absorb a lot of energy are connected.

The armrest and boot house two power points that can be used to power various appliances.

The Body Computer manages lighting of the cigar lighter, armrest power opint and boot power point with the side lights on;

See E2530 INSTRUMENT LIGHTING

the power point/cigar lighter and boot power point circuits are protected by a specific fuse and controlled by an ignition-operated cut-out relay located in the under-facia junction unit. The power point on the armrest is protected by a specific fuse and controlled by a service relay contained inside the boot junction unit.

The devices are operational with key-on (INT/A).
