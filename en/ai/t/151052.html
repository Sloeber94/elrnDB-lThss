Refitting

- Open the boot and disconnect the negative battery terminal.

See in Op. 5580P24 CONNECT CONTROL UNIT SUPPORT - R.R.

-

it is advisable to place the gear engagement control lever as far back as possible to prevent it interfering with the procedure operations.

1. Acting on the side retaining clips, release the connect control unit from the mounting bracket and extract it

2. Disconnect the electrical connectors (2a), (2b), (2c) and (2d).
