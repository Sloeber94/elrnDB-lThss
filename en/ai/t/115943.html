7055B64 Engine compartment DUST SEAL in front wheel arch (one), left or right - R.R.

Interactive page

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

See in Op. 4450B04 Right FRONT WHEEL (one) - R.R.

-

See in Op. 7055B42 FRONT WHEEL ARCH LINER (one), left or right - R.R.

1. Undo the bolts fixing the dust seal to the shield under the engine.

2. Undo the bolts fixing the dust seal to the bodyshell (2a) and to the bumper (2b).

3. Remove the dust seal from the wheel housing.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the dust seal in the wheel housing and secure it to the bodyshell, the bumper and the shield under the engine using the bolts.

See in Op. 7055B42 FRONT WHEEL ARCH LINER (one), left or right - R.R.

See in Op. 4450B04 Right FRONT WHEEL (one) - R.R.

-

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.
