Applies to: all engines; all production periods

One of the two side direction indicators remains constantly on or off;

The direction indicators are controlled and diagnosed (lights check function) directly by the Body Computer NodeIf there is a fault with the direction indicators confirmed by the Body Computer Node, the fault is displayed on the matrix that can be reconfigured

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

BULB CONDITION CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Extract the side light from its housing and check the condition of the bulb

Move on to Step

Bulb burnt out

Replace the side repeaterSee in Op. 5550C22 Left or right SIDE REPEATER (one) - R.R.

2

ELECTRICAL SYSTEM CHECK.

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the continuity of the wiring between the side light and the Body Computer Node. (Wiring Diagram ) E2020 DIRECTION INDICATORS/HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS

Move on to Step

Wiring broken

Restore the electrical connections. (Wiring Diagram ) E2020 DIRECTION INDICATORS/HAZARD WARNING LIGHTS

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION.

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists

Replace body computerSee in Op. 5505A32 BODY COMPUTER - R.R.
