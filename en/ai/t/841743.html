Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

The system consists of:

  • a Pierburg EGR solenoid valve (1)
  • the pipe coming from the exhaust manifold (from where the exhaust gases arrive)
  • the pipe connected to the intake (from where the exhaust gases are introduced).
