Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the desired passenger side temperature is set, there is no corresponding flow of air at the required temperature.

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket and check:for faults in the climate control system control unit

Move on to Step

1. Presence of faults

1. Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

2. No communication with the control unit

2. Restore the connections between the control unit and the diagnostic socket(Wiring Diagram ) E6020 AIR CONDITIONING

2

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS WITH DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) and carry out the active diagnosis of the right mixing motor

Move on to Step

Mixing motor not working

Move on to Step

3

PASSENGER SIDE MIXING MOTOR ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check:the connection between the climate control system control unit and the mixing motor

Move on to Step

1. Connections not correct

1. Restore the correct connection(Wiring Diagram ) E6020 AIR CONDITIONING

2. Connections correct, mixing motor faulty

2. Check the condition of the flap, if necessary, replace the motorSee in Op. 5040D86 air conditioner right air mixture flap control motor - r r
