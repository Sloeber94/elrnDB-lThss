C38 IMPOSSIBLE TO EXTRACT THE KEY FROM THE LOCK BARREL

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Automatic Transmission

Impossible to extract the ignition key from the lock barrel with the panel off. After switching the engine and the panel off, it is impossible to extract the key from the ignition.

After the key-off, the control unit remains active for 25 seconds; within this time the gear lever must be placed in P to deactivate the key-lock function and extract the key
