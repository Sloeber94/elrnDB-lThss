- Lower the vehicle

- Position the bowden cable starting from the engine compartment and securing the end to the gear selector.

- Raise the vehicle

- Connect the end of the bowden cable to the idler on the lever support

- Close the lower lever support cover, making sure that all the retaining springs are correctly attached

- Tighten the nut fixing the bowden cable mounting bracket to the bodyshell to the recommended torque.
