Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The effect of the supplementary heater cannot be felt under the specified operating conditions;

: activation of the supplementary heater in this mode is managed automatically by the climate control system mode and permitted only the following conditions occur simultaneously:: activation of the supplementary heater in this mode is managed automatically by the climate control system mode and permitted only the following conditions occur simultaneously:1 key-on position2 coolant temperature lower than 30 c°3 interior temperature requirement higher than measured interior temperature4 engine rpm higher than 700 rpmFuel pump or coolant pump activation failure is diagnosed by the supplementary heater control unitFuel pump or coolant pump activation failure is diagnosed by the supplementary heater control unit
