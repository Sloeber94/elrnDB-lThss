Refitting

Removing

- Fit a new left cylinder head gasket.

The cylinder head gasket is the Astadur type. The material from which the gasket is made undergoes a polymerization process during the operation of the engine so that it becomes considerably harder during usage. In order for the polymerization process to take place, it is necessary to: - keep the gasket sealed in its container until it is fitted; - do not lubricate the gasket or contact surfaces or get oil on them.

Two mechanics are needed for this stage

- Position the cylinder head on the cylinder block/crankcase.

1. Tighten the nuts (1a) fixing the cylinder head to the recommended torque, in stages, using the angular tightening tool (1b).
