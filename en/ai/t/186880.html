1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the left rear timing belt guard (1b).

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the left cylinder head camshaft caps (2b).

3. Remove the left cylinder head camshafts complete with front oil seals.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods
