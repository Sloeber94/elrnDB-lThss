Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

- Open the boot, remove the cover on the left hand side and disconnect the negative battery lead.

3. Disconnect the electrical connection for the engine coolant level.

1.870.828.000

Engine tuning

Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB; all production periods

1. Fit the timing toothed drive belt in the following order:

- toothed drive pulley (1a)

- fixed tensioner (1b)

- exhaust side toothed driven pulley (1c)

- inlet side toothed driven pulley (1d)

- moving tensioner (1e)

- water pump pulley (1f).

The belt is fitted with the arrow facing the direction of rotation of the engine. There are also three references on the belt that should be aligned, during the initial fitting, with the references on the toothed drive pulley and on the inlet and exhaust side toothed driven pullies.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB; all production periods

1. Using a screwdriver, apply force to the tab (1a) for the automatic tensioner so that the tensioner is in the maximum tension position, then tighten the nut (1b) fixing the tensioner to the support.
