Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

See in Op. 7055B54 PROTECTION/GUARD under engine - R.R.

1. Remove the engine oil filter (1a) using a suitable tool (1b).

The engine oil and the filter that have been replaced contain substances that are dangerous to the environment. The oil and the filters should be deposited in specially designed containers.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB, 2.4 20V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Lubricate the new filter gasket with engine oil.

- Fit the engine oil cartridge filter, complete with gasket, and tighten it fully, by hand.
