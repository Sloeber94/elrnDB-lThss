Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB, 2.4 20V, 2.4 JTD 10V, 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

During acceleration there is a consisten increase in the engine revs without a corresponding increase in speed

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

0

PRELIMINARY CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

With the engine running, press the clutch pedal and engage 4th gear. Accelerate and gradually release the clutch pedal, checking that the engine revs not increase during this manoeuvre whilst the vehicle remains stationary.

Move on to Step

The vehicle does not move and the engine revs increase

Move on to Step

1

CLUTCH - FLYWHEEL ASSEMBLY CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:clucth plate wearclutch plate foulingno distortion of the clutch platecondition of the diaphragm springcondition of the flywheel (in particular the state of the surface, uneven wear, signs of overheating)

Move on to Step

1. Disc worn

1. Replace the assemblySee in Op. 1810A20 CLUTCH - R.R.

2. Disc fouled with oil or grease

2. Replace the clutch plate, clean the assembly, eliminate the oil leaksSee in Op. 1810A24 CLUTCH - R.R. with gearbox removed

3. Disc distorted

3. Replace the assemblySee in Op. 1810A24 CLUTCH - R.R. with gearbox removed

4. Spring broken

4. Replace the assemblySee in Op. 1810A24 CLUTCH - R.R. with gearbox removed

5. Flywheel scored, worn or overheated

5. Replace the flywheelSee in Op. 1024B22 ENGINE FLYWHEEL - R.R. with clutch removed for versions with manual gearbox

2

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present
