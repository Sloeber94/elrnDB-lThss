Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the alarm is switched on using the remote control, the system is activated correctly but the volumetric sensors do not intervene.

The volumetric sensors are located in the roof node and are connected to the Body Computer Node via the A-BUS dialogue line.The volumetric sensors are operated and controlled by the Body Computer Node via a series of encrypted messages and can be excluded by the customer using the special button.If there is a failure the Body Computer Node detects and memorizes the causes of the malfunction
