Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods

See in Op. 1032B10 TOOTHED timing BELT - R.R. for timing adjustment or replacement

- Instead of only loosening toothed drive pulley bolt ( < R1 > ), unscrew completely and remove the pulley.

- Refit the toothed driven pulley in its seat and secure with the bolt without tightening.
