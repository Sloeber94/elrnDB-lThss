Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition in the ON position, when the button is operated, one of the front electric windows is not working at all.

each of the front windows is controlled by a specific door node; the driver's door node, in particular, can also control the movement of the passenger front window via the B-CAN.

ALL OK

ACTION

1. Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment
