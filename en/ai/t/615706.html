Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB; all production periods

STRUCTURE

The power unit is said to be suspended.

The unit is secured to the body by means of 4 mounts with flexible damping blocks:

  • one on aluminium alloy on the camshaft side;
  • a rear steel block that is secured to the suspension beam;
  • one on aluminium alloy on the gearbox side;
  • one to limit engine shake that is connected to the body at the top by means of a reaction link.
