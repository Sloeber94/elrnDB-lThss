Applies to: engines: 2.4 20V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the piston-connecting rod assemblies, complete with half-bearings, in their housings.

Use a suitable tool for compressing the seals and then fitting in the cylinder liner/bore.

The pistons and connecting rods are fitted in the cylinder block/crankcase so that the large references (for the inlet valves) in the piston crown are facing the inlet side.

- Place the connecting rod caps complete with half bearings in their housings and secure them tightening the bolts to the recommended torque.

Fit the connecting rods so that the number stamped on each rod faces towards the same side as the number stamped on the big end.

Value - daNm
