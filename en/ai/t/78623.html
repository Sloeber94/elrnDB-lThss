3330D24 vacuum pipe for brake servo - r+r

Applies to: engines: 2.4 20V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the fixing bolt (1a) and move the wiring holder bracket (1b) to the side.

2. Remove the one-way valve from the brake servo which is a press-fit.

3. Disconnect the vacuum pipe from the housing on the modular intake manifold, then remove the pipe.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 20V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the vacuum pipe in the housing on the modular intake manifold and secure it using the band.

- Connect the one-way valve (which is a press-fit) to the brake servo.

- Position the wiring holder bracket and tighten the fixing bolt.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 10V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Open the bonnet

1. Open the band (1a) fastening the pipe (1c) to the intake chamber (1b) and disconnect the pipe.

2. Release the brake servo vacuum pipe from the vaccum increase valve and remove it.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the pipe on the vacuum increase valve making sure that it is correctly fitted.

- Fit the pipe on the intake chamber and close the retaining band.

- Close the bonnet

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20V; all production periods
