1040A70 SUBMERGED PUMP ASSEMBLY complete with level gauge - R.R.

Removing

Refitting

- Open the boot, remove the cover on the left hand side and disconnect the negative battery lead.

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the hooks (1b).

2. Remove the luggage compartment cover.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 20V; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the access cover (1b) for the submerged pump assembly complete with level gauge.

2. Disconnect the electrical connection from the submerged pump assembly complete with level gauge.
