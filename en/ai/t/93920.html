1076B68 EXHAUST PIPE FLEXIBLE SECTION - R.R.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 20V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

See in Op. 7055B54 PROTECTION/GUARD under engine - R.R.

1. Undo the nuts fixing the catalytic converter to the centre section of the exhaust pipe.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) fixing the section centre to the rigid rear section (2b).

3. Release the pins (3a) from the pipe rubber mountings (3b).

4. Remove the centre section of the exhaust pipe, saving the gaskets underneath.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 20V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the seal in the special housing in the catalyzer.

- Secure the pins to the rubber mountings.

- Tighten the nuts fixing the catalytic converter to the centre section of the exhaust pipe.

Value - daNm
