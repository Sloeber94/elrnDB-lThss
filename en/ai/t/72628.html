Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition on, the display either does not show the time or it is not correct;

the interior lights on signal is sent from the "body computer node", via the B-CAN, to the "instrument panel node".

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic connector and check the following at the CAN: no control unit not activated;that there are no faulty control units;no control unit with faulty CAN line;

Move on to Step

Presence of faults

Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

2

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Keeping the Examiner diagnostic equipment connected to the diagnostic connector, check the following at the instrument panel node:in active diagnosis ambientthe operation of the panel lighting.

Move on to Step

The instrument panel does not light up

Replace instrument panelSee in Op. 5560B11 ANALOGUE CONTROL PANEL - R.R. or Replace

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present
