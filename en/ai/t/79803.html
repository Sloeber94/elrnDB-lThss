Removing

Refitting

1. Detach the protective water shield and remove it.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the new protection is intact and not torn.

Do not use the protection that was removed from the door.

- Clean the surface of the bodyshell involved thoroughly using heptane.

- Position the new water shield, taking care to ensure that the adhesive sticks to the door frame.
