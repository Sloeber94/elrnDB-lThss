Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The Servotronik is a device that allows a gradual hardening of the movement of the steering wheel and and consequently improved road holding as the speed of the vehicle increases.

The funciton is entirely managed by a special Servotronik control unit that manages:

  • the speedometer signal supplied by the braking system control unit;
  • the supply of the servotronik solenoid valve that allows the operation of the device;
  • its own diagnostic signalSee E8010 DIAGNOSTIC MULTIPLE CONNECTOR
  • a device failure signal, acquired directly from the instrument panel which shows the fault message in the display.

The Servotronik control unit receives a power supply from the line controlled by the ignition (INT).
