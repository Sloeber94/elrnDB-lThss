Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Open the boot, remove the cover on the left hand side and disconnect the negative battery lead.

See in Op. 7040D50 ENGINE COMPARTMENT UPPER SOUND INSULATION SHIELD - R.R.

See in Op. 1048A28 vacuum air circuit resonator pipe - r+r

See in Op. 1048A21 AIR INLET HOSE FROM INTAKE TO FILTER - R.R.

1. Disconnect the electrical connection from the air flow meter.

2. Release the air filter casing from the two flexible mountings, then remove the air filter (2a), complete with air flow meter (2b).

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the air filter, complete with air flow meter, in its housing and fasten it to the two flexible mountings.

Take care to ensure that the filter element is correctly positioned during the refitting, checking that the seal is not coming out of its housing and that it is not damaged.

Clean the air flow meter thoroughly to avoid damaging the sensitive element.

- Connect the electrical connection to the air flow meter.

See in Op. 1048A21 AIR INLET HOSE FROM INTAKE TO FILTER - R.R.

See in Op. 1048A28 vacuum air circuit resonator pipe - r+r

See in Op. 7040D50 ENGINE COMPARTMENT UPPER SOUND INSULATION SHIELD - R.R.

- Connect the negative battery lead, then close the boot.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB; all production periods

- Operation not available
