Removing

Refitting

1. Disconnect the oil vapour recovery pipe from the connector on the hose.

2. Disconnect the pipe (2a) from the air flow meter (2b) and from the turbocharger air intake hose (2c), then remove it.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 10V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the air inlet pipe and connect it to the air flow meter and to the turbocharger air intake hose.

- Connect the engine oil vapour recovery pipe to the air inlet pipe.
