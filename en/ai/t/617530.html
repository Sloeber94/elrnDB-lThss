CONNECTING RODS

Specifications

Type

The connecting rods are made out of hardened steel with a copper alloy bush (1) press-fitted for couping with piston pin (2).

The floating pins are held in place by two expansion rings (3) that are housed in grooves on the piston hubs.

PISTONS

Specifications

Type

The pistons in aluminium alloy with autothermal inserts (1) are divided into three size categories and bear an arrow stamped on the crown to indicate engine rotation direction to ensure correct installation.

The piston crown is concave with four grooves to prevent interference with the valve heads.
