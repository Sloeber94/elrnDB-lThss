Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB, 2.4 20V, 3.0 V6, 2.4 JTD 10V; all production periods

The MIL warning light does not come with the ignition on;

the MIL warning light is switched on by means of an earth command directly from the "engine management node"; during normal operation the MIL warning light should come on when the ignition is on and go out when the engine is started up.
