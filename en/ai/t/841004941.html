Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The connect system for displaying messages and various settings offered by the info-telematic control unit consists of a direct connection which is connected by means of two connectors to the front panel in the dashboard.

In addition to providing the supply and earth connection for the front panel, the info-telematic control unit also manages the logic for the messages shown on the display.
