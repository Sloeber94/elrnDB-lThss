B04 STARTER MOTOR NOT WORKING (vehicle with passive entry)

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Passive Entry

The CID is recognized, the steering lock is released, but the starter motor does not work

the enabling of the starter relay only takes place in the following conditions:the enabling of the starter relay only takes place in the following conditions:1. Starting control activated2. Motor enablement received by the network coming from the Body Computer Node3. Clutch/brake pressed when the previous two conditions are verified.
