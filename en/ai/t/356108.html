Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The additional heater is programmed by the Connect, however the heater does not come on at the time set.

during programming the Connect informs the driver of the correct enabling of the timer;in this way the heater on signal is sent by the Connect to the additional heater control unit via the climate control node.
