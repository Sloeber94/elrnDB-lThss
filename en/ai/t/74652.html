Removing

Refitting

1. Undo and remove the engine oil filler plug.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the ignition coil cover on the cylinder head (2b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB, 2.4 20V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the ignition coil cover on the cylinder head back in its housing and secure it using the bolts.
