1068A20 ACCELERATOR PEDAL WITH INTEGRATED POTENTIOMETER - R.R.

Path
Procedures > 10 ENGINE > 1068 accelerator controls > 1068A accelerator pedal control > 1068A20 ACCELERATOR PEDAL WITH INTEGRATED POTENTIOMETER - R.R.
Kind
repair procedure (removal / refitting; the operation number is the code)
Applies to
engines: 2.0 TB, 2.4 20V, 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods
Interactive page
https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB-lThss/en/#/93727

Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB, 2.4 20V; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

1. Disconnect the electrical connection for the accelerator pedal potentiometer.

Description

Connector

1

Accelerator pedal potentiometer

K055

2. Undo the bolts fixing the pedal to the floor.

3. Remove the accelerator pedal with the integrated potentiometer.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB, 2.4 20V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the accelerator pedal with the integrated potentiometer.

- Tighten the bolts fixing the pedal to the floor.

- Connect the electrical connection for the accelerator pedal potentiometer.

Description

Connector

.

Accelerator pedal potentiometer

K055

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods
