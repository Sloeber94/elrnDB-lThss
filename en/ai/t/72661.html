Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the ignition is in the ON position, when the buttons located in the doors are operated the electric front windows do not work at all.

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Using the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) connected, check the following information at the CAN network:no control unit not activated;that there are no faulty control units;no control unit with faulty CAN line;

Move on to Step

1. Presence of faults

1. Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

2. Driver's/passenger door node not activated

Move on to Step

2

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

Check the condition of the fuses in the luggage compartment junction unit(Wiring Diagram ) E3050 FRONT ELECTRIC WINDOWS

Fuse damaged

Replace the fuse

ELECTRICAL SYSTEM CHECK.

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED
