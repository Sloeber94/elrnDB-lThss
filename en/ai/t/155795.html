Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the connectors (1a) joining the front sections to the rear sections and also remove the fastening bands (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the connector joining the rigid section to the flexible section of the right rear brake pipe.

- Repeat this operation on the left brake caliper pipe.

2. Remove the rigid pipe assemblies.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the two rigid pipes to the rear brakes and tighten the connectors for the flexible sections to the brake calipers.
