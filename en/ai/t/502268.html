Refitting

1. Remove the piston rings.

2. Release the pin (2a) and separate the connecting rods (2b) from the pistons (2c).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Join the connecting rods with the pistons so that the number stamped on the big end (1a) is facing the combustion chamber (1b) in the piston.

- Fit the pins and secure them using the circlips.
