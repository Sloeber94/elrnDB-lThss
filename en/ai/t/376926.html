Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Whilst the steering is operated there is a noise when it reaches the end of travel position.

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Visually inspect that the level of the power assisted steering in the reservoir is correct; conduct a test steering completely and then releasing the steering wheel

Move on to Step

1. Level too low

1. Restore the correct level; if there are leaks, eliminate them

2. The noise increases towards the end of travel position and with the engine speed

Move on to Step

3. The noise remains constant (hissing whilst steering, puffing on release)

3. No intervention is nececssary: the noise is due to the by-pass valve that discharges the excess pressure

1

POWER ASSISTED STEERING FLUID QUALITY CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Visually inspect the power assisted steering fluid in the reservoir. Make sure that there is no emulsified fluid (fluid that is lighter in colour with bubbles)

Move on to Step

Emulsified fluid

Eliminate any air penetraion through the power assisted steering system pipes

2

POWER ASSISTED STEERING PIPES CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for constrictions in the system flexible and rigid pipes

Move on to Step

Pipes faulty

Replace the damaged parts, replacing all the seals

3

POWER ASSISTED STEERING PUMP CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the power assisted steering pump is working properly

Move on to Step

Power assisted steering pump malfunction

Replace the power assisted steering pumpSee in Op. 4130B20 POWER ASSISTED STEERING PUMP - R.R.

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present
