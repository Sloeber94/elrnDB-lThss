Applies to: all engines; all production periods

While driving, the speedometer does not work or works irregularly;

the speed signal is supplied by the "brake node" and made available to the "instrument panel node" via the "body computer node" Gateway. Remember that if the C-CAN network is faulty, the signal is made available on the B-CAN network from the Body Computer node via recovery line between brake node and body computer node known as VSO
