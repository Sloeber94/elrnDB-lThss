1. Undo the two bolts (1a) fixing the rigid section (1b) of the alternator cooling pipe.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the tappet cover, right side (2b), complete with gasket.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the right side tappet cover, complete with a new gasket, and secure it tightening the bolts to the recommended torque taking care to position rigid section of the alternator cooling pipe before tightening the bolts.
