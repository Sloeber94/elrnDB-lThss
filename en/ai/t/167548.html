Applies to: all engines; all production periods

One of the two main beam headlamps is not working or is working irregularly;

The main beam headlamps are controlled by the Body Computer Node via a relay located in the Engine Compartment Junction Unit.The main beam headlamp consists of a traditional bulb (known as the "auxiliary headlamp") and an electronic module connected in parallel to the bulb which supplies a coil which activates a partition which fully releases the xenon dipped headlamp.
