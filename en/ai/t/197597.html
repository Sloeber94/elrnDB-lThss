Refitting

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Remove the retaining seals (1a) and release the cover (1b) from the cushion upholstery (1c).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Detach the heater pad and remove it.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the heater pad is not damaged.

- Replace the double-sided tape on the heater pad.

- Position the heater pad and fasten the adhesive side to the cushion upholstery.

- Fit the cover on the cushion upholstery and secure it using the retaining seals.
