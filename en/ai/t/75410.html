4. Secure the chains to the hydraulic lifting device and tension the engine.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 10V; all production periods

- Raise the power unit tilting the gearbox unit downwards, rotate the assembly so that the timing side is towards the front of the vehicle then, using the hydraulic lift, position the power unit on a suitable support.

Take great care not to damage the components fitted on the vehicle whilst rotating and removing the engine. Whilst rotating the power unit check that the gearbox-differential unit does not come into contact with the ABS unit; if necessary, disconnect the electrical connector for the ABS electro-hydraulic control unit.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 10V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Support the power unit using the hydraulic lift fitted with suitable chains and position the power unit in the special housing in the vehicle.

- Remove the chains, hooks and brackets fitted for supporting the power unit.

- Fit the gearbox side engine support.

- Tighten the bolts fixing the rigid support to the bellhousing to torque.
