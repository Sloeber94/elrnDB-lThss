7045B08 Front seat head restraint pins - R.R.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

See in Op. 7045A10 FRONT SEAT (one) left or right - R.R.

See in Op. 7045J10 switch unit for movement of one front seat, l/h or r/h - r+r for check

See in Op. 7045A42 Front seat GUARD (one), left or right - R.R.

- [all the elements]

See in Op. 7045A98 Front seat rear pouch panel, left or right - R.R.

See in Op. 7045B12 Front seat head restraint - R.R. with seat removed

1. Press the retaining tab (1a) and release the head restraint pin (1b) from the housing.

- Carry out the same operation for the remaining pin.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Fit the head restraint pin (1a) which has the rear centering device (1b), in the housing on the right seat.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Fit the other pin.

See in Op. 7045B12 Front seat head restraint - R.R. with seat removed

See in Op. 7045A98 Front seat rear pouch panel, left or right - R.R.

See in Op. 7045A42 Front seat GUARD (one), left or right - R.R.

- [all the elements]

See in Op. 7045J10 switch unit for movement of one front seat, l/h or r/h - r+r for check

See in Op. 7045A10 FRONT SEAT (one) left or right - R.R.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.
