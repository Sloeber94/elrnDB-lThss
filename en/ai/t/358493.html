Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The alignment of the headlamps is corrected automatically and continuously by a Headlamp Alignment Corrector (C.A.F.) control unit;the correction of the headlamp alignment is activated by switching the dipped headlamps on;the C.A.F. control unit has an autodiagnostic function and, if there are errors in the various components, it controls the warning light (matrix message) in the Instrument Panel Node.
