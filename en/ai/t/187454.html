Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

- Open the boot, remove the cover on the left hand side and disconnect the negative battery lead.

- Raise the vehicle

1. Undo the nuts (1a) fixing the flanges for the pipes (1b) between the right preconverter and the converter and remove the actual pipe complete with gaskets underneath.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 V6, 3.2 V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Position the pipe between the right preconverter and the converter, complete with gaskets, then tighten the nuts (1a) fixing the pipe (1b) flanges to the recommended torque.
