Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 10V; all production periods

TYPE

Current developments

These engines are fitted with a high pressure electronic injection system for fast diesel engines. This 'Bosch Common Rail EDC-15C7-2.2' system is fully managed by an electronic control unit.

Specifications

5 cylinder in line, 10 valve, 2386 cc turbodiesel engine with Bosch Common Rail Unijet EDC 15C - 7-2.2 direct injection

POWER - 150 BHP EC (110 kW) at 4000 rpm

TORQUE - 31.10 kgm EC ( 305 Nm) at 1800 rpm

CYLINDER ARRANGEMENT - 5 in line

BORE - 82 mm

STROKE - 90.4 mm

CYLINDER CAPACITY - 2386 cc

CYLINDER HEAD - aluminium alloy

CYLINDER BLOCK/CRANKACSE - cast iron closed deck

CRANKSHAFT - ductile cast iron with 6 main bearings

TIMING SYSTEM - single overhead camshaft, belt driven, 2 valves per cylinder

FUEL SYSTEM - unijet direct injection with variable geometry turbocharger and intercooler

IGNITION - Bosch EDC 15C- 7-2.2. electronic

EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES - E.G.R. with catalytic converter

LUBRICATION - forced with geared pump and water/oil radiator

COOLING - liquid with forced circulation via centrifugal pump

SYSTEM DETAILS

Devices adopted

To reduce polluting emission levels, these engines are equipped with:

  • exhaust system with oxidising catalytic converter
  • Exhaust gas recirculation system (E.G.R.)
  • crankcase blowby gas recirculation system.
