Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 10V; all production periods

FUNCTION

The air capacity chamber is in aluminium. It receives pressurised air cooled by the intercooler and carries it directly to the cylinder head.

The spiral intake ducts impose the swirling motion on the air required for efficient combustion.

Excess pressure sensor [DF 1060G] connected to the injection control unit is fitted to the chamber.
