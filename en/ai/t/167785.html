Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 10V; all production periods

The matrix shows "low engine oil level" information that does not correspond to the level shown on the dipstick;

the engine oil level information is manageed by the Instrument Panel Node by means of an oil level sensor on the engine connected directly to the Instrument Panel Node.
