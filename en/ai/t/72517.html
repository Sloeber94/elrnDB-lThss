Applies to: all engines; all production periods

One of the two main beam headlamps is not working or is working irregularly;

The rear fog lamps are controlled and diagnosed directly by the Body Computer Node.Any fault detected by the Body Computer Node is displayed on the reconfigurable matrix.

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT.

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment to the diagnostic socket and check the following at the Body Computer Node:that there are no faults at the rear fog lampsthe operation of the rear fog lampsthe plausibility of the Rear Fog Lamps On/Off parameter.

Move on to Step

1. Error at the rear fog lamps

1. Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

2. The lights do not come on

Move on to Step

3.The rear fog lamps on/off parameter does not alter

Move on to Step

2

REAR FOG LAMPS ON COMMAND CHECK.

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the operation of the rear fog lamps on control at the exterior lights controlContinue following the instructions in the test 5540AA Exterior lights control operational check (C.L.E.)

Move on to Step

On/off button faulty

Replace the exterior lights controlSee in Op. 5040A20 Air conditioning COMPRESSOR - R.R. - Includes fluid level check and top up if necessary

3

ELECTRICAL SYSTEM CHECK.

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED
