Removing

Refitting

See in Op. 1016A40 Ignition COIL COVER on cylinder head - R.R.

1. Remove the band (1a) on the (V.I.S.) device with adjustable air ducts and the band (1b) on the tappet cover.

2. Remove the idle engine oil vapour inlet pipe.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 20V; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the idle engine oil vapour inlet pipe.

- Fasten the pipe ton the (V.I.S.) device with adjustable air ducts and to the tappet cover using the bands.

See in Op. 1016A40 Ignition COIL COVER on cylinder head - R.R.
