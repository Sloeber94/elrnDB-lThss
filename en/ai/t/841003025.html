Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

Open the rear armrest flap.

1. Undo the armrest insert retaining bolts.

2. Disconnect the electrical connections for the armrest switches.

3. Remove the rear armrest insert.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Connect the electrical connections for the armrest switches.

Fit the rear armrest insert.

Tighten the fixing bolts.

Connect the negative battery terminal.
