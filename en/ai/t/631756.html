Applies to: engines: 2.4 20V; all production periods

TYPE

Current developments

This engine has a Bosch Motronic electronic injection system managed entirely by an electronic control unit.

Specifications

5 cylinder in-line engine, 20 valves, 2386 cc., petrol, with integrated Bosch ME 3.1 electronic injection-ignition system

POWER - 125 kW (170 BHP)

TORQUE - 23.04 kgm (226 Nm)

CYLINDER ARRANGEMENT - 5 in line

BORE - 82 mm

STROKE - 90.4 mm

CYLINDER CAPACITY - 2386 cc

CYLINDER HEAD - in heated-treated aluminium

CRANKCASE - in laminar cast iron

CRANKSHAFT - in spheroidal cast iron

TIMING SYSTEM - two overhead camshafts, 4 valves per cylinder, hydraulic tappets

FUEL SYSTEM - returnless system with Bosch injection

IGNITION - single coils per cylinder

EMISSION CONTROL DEVICES - Euro 3 with catalytic converter

LUBICATION - forced with gear pump

COOLING - liquid with forced circulation via centrifugal pump and sealed circuit; radiator and supplementary expansion tank

SYSTEM DETAILS

Devices adopted

The following systems are fitted on these engine to keep down levels of pollutant emissions:

  • exhaust system with catalytic converter and Lambda sensors;
  • fuel vapour recirculation system.
  • crankcase vapour/gas recirculation system.
