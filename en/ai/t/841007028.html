Description

The vehicle is equipped with a radio incorpoted in the integrated radio-telephone-navigation system.

A sophisticated Bose HIFI system, equipped with special hifi speakers and a DSP (digital signal processing) amplifier is available on request.

The system has been specially developed for this vehicle so that the amplifier and the speakers offer the best output inside the vehicle and excellent listening anywhere in the passenger compartment.

The Bose system contains the following components:

  • radio;
  • Bose amplifier, located in the luggage compartment;
  • centre front speaker;
  • speakers in the rear parcel shelf (left and right);
  • front speakers (separate woofers and tweeters) located in the doors;
  • rear speakers (separate woofers and tweeters) located in the doors;
  • radio supply leads;
  • cables connecting the radio with the speakers;
  • AM/FM aerial, coaxial cables and supply lead for the aerial amplifier

The operating modes and the controls are identical to the basic system.
