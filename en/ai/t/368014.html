Applies to: all engines; all production periods

the external rear view mirrors are controlled directly by the Door Nodes according to information received from the joystick on the left front door handle.the mirror folding information for the Driver's Door Node is direct, whilst for the Passenger Dor Node the information is made available by the Driver's Door Node via the B-CAN.
