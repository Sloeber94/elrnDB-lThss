Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the vehicle is moving there is a smell of exhaust gases coming from the rear area

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

EXHAUST SILENCER/PIPE CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the exhaust silencers and pipes and make sure there are no leaks

Move on to Step

Pipes/silencers leaking

Replace the pipes/silencers

2

BOOT LID SEAL CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the seals. In particular, check that the seals are smooth

Move on to Step

Seals not fitted correctly or seals defective

Make sure that the seals are fitted correctly and, if necessary, replace them

3

BOOT LID ALIGNMENT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the alignment, checking that the seals are in contact with the entire perimeter

Move on to Step

Boot lid not correctly fitted

Refit the boot lid correctly

4

BOOT HOUSING SEAL CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the boot housing seal is correct

Move on to Step

Boot housing seal faulty

Check that the boot housing seal is correct

5

UNDERBODY CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the underbody plugs are fitted

Move on to Step

No underbody plugs

Fit the missing plugs

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present
