Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Before carrying out the operations described below, raise the seat cushion until it is in the end of travel position.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

See in Op. 7045A10 FRONT SEAT (one) left or right - R.R.

See in Op. 7045J10 switch unit for movement of one front seat, l/h or r/h - r+r for check

See in Op. 7045A42 Front seat GUARD (one), left or right - R.R.

- < R1 > [all the elements]

- Disconnect the electrical connector for the front seat cushion electric lifting motor.

1. Release the retaining spring.

2. Release the fastening pins (2a) and (2b) from the housing and remove the electric motor (2c).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the electric motor in position and secure it using the fastening pins.

- Fit the retaining spring.

- Connect the electrical connector for the front seat cushion electric lifting motor.

See in Op. 7045A42 Front seat GUARD (one), left or right - R.R.

- < R1 > [all the elements]

See in Op. 7045J10 switch unit for movement of one front seat, l/h or r/h - r+r for check

See in Op. 7045A10 FRONT SEAT (one) left or right - R.R.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

- Check that the motor is working properly.
