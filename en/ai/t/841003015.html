Op. 7045A10 L/H OR R/H FRONT SEAT (ONE) - R+R

Op. 7045J10 SWITCH UNIT FOR MOVEMENT OF ONE FRONT SEAT, L/H OR R/H - R+R FOR CHECK

Op. 7045A42 L/H OR R/H FRONT SEAT GUARD (ONE) - R+R

[left side and centre]

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Remove the connector holder for the front seat.

2. Undo the nut (2a) and remove the relay holder (2b).

Open the retaining bands for the wiring.

3. Disconnect the electrical connections (3a) for the seat/mirror with memory control unit, the front seat adjustment motors, the heater pad, the coupling with the backrest and remove the front seat movement system cable loom (3b).

Disconnect the electrical connections for the electro-pneumatic control unit and cooling fans.

Disconnect the connection for the passenger presence sensor on the passenger seat.
