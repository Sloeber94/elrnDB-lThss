FUEL FILTER

Specifications

Type

The fuel filter is fitted in the engine bay on the bulkhead.

It is cartridge type with a filter element (1) consisting of a paper disc pack with a filtering area of 5300 cm² and a filtering level of 4 - 5 microns.

Plug (2) fitted at the base of the cartridge allows water to drain from the filter.
