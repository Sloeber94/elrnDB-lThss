1. Undo the injector bracket retaining nuts.

2. Remove the injectors (2a) complete with brackets using the tools (2b) and (2c).

If the injectors are being reused, mark the cylinder to which they belong so that they can be correctly refitted. If new injector(s) is(are) being fitted, it is necessary to:

    Start the removal beginning with the injector for cylinder no. 1 and continue with the injectors for cylinders 2, 3, 4 and 5.
