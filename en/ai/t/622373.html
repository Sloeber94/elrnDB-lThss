Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB; all production periods

FUNCTION

The air capacity chamber is made out of aluminium. It receives pressurised air, cooled by the intercooler, and conveys it directly to the cylinder head.

The spiral intake ducts impose a swirling motion on the air, which is required for efficient combustion.
