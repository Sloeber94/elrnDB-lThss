7210E18 FRONT WING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The vehicle should be in the following condition before the operation: front headlamp removed, side warning light removed, bonnet open.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Check that the connected components are not distorted by checking the bodyshell alignment figures using suitable equipment (gauges, templates, jigs).

1. Undo the upper retaining bolts.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the wheel arch fixing bolts (1a) and remove the wing (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the wing and tighten the upper fixing bolts.

- Tighten the wheel arch fixing bolts.

When the work is completed, refit the components removed previously.
