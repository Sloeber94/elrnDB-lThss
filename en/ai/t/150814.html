Before carrying out the operations described below, the following preliminary operations must be carried out: (1) turn the ignition key to the OFF position; (2) disconnect and insulate the battery leads; (3) wait for 10 minutes before continuing.

FOLLOW ALL THE RECOMMENDED SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS METICULOUSLY (See assembly 5580C AIR BAG SYSTEM)
