I17 REVERSING LIGHTS NOT WORKING

Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB, 2.4 20V, 3.0 V6, 2.4 JTD 10V; all production periods

When reverse gear is engaged the reversing warning lights do not work;

The reversing lights are controlled directly by the switch on the gearbox.The Body Computer Node receives a lights on signal from the positive supply for the reversing light bulbs.
