Refitting

Removing

- Replace the old filter element with a new one which has the same specifications.

- Fit the new filter element in the filter casing.

Take care to ensure that the filter element is correctly positioned during the refitting, checking that the seal is not coming out of its housing and that it is not damaged.

- Tighten the bolts fixing the air filter casing cover.

Clean the air flow meter thoroughly to avoid damaging the sensitive element.

- Connect the air flow meter connector.

- Connect the air inlet pipe to the air flow meter.
