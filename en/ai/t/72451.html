Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The vehicle vibrates and the pedal oscillates during braking

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

DISC CENTERING CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Remove the wheels and check that the disc offset is within the recommended values (see technical data) and that there is no clearance in the wheel hubs

Move on to Step

1. The offset is too high, there is no clearance in the wheel hubs

1. Regrind or, if necessary, replace the discs on the same axle

2. The offset is too high and there is clearance in the wheel hubs

2. Regrind or, if necessary, replace the discs and the wheel bearings on the same axle

2

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Test the vehicle before returning it to the customer, checking that the problem no longer exists. The test is essential becaues the braking system is one of the vehicle's safety systems
