Applies to: all engines; all production periods

There is a sensor on every tyre valve that measures the pressure and the temperature of the actual tyre: the electronic control unit manages and controls the system and sends the Body Computer the following information via the serial line: the "insufficient pressure" signal with the reading for the tyre involved is sent via the CAN to the instrument panel which activates the related signals.

The control unit supply lines are protected by fuses in the underfacia control unit.
