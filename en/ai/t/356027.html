L26 (ELECTRIC) AIR PREHEATING IS NOT ACTIVATED AT LOW OUTSIDE TEMPERATURES

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 10V; all production periods

The outside temperature information is correctly displayed on the reconfigurable matrix.

The electrical pre-heating device (only fitted on the JTD versions) consists of two (PTC) resistances with different resistance values which develop a maximum power of 700 W when a current passes through; when one of the two resistances or both of them are activated, the climate control system control unit is capable of modulating the interior heating;

the system can only be activated in the following circumstances:the system can only be activated in the following circumstances:1) Ignition on and climate control switched on2) Outside temperature below 20°C3) Coolant temperature below 64°C4) Battery voltage not less than 12.2 V5) Engine rpm higher than 700 rpm
