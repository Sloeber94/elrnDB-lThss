Applies to: engines: 2.4 20V; all production periods

STRUCTURE

The unit is system comprising the engine and all the systems designed for its operation:

  • fuel supply syste
  • fuel supply system
  • exhaust system with catalytic converter and Lambda sensors
  • oil vapour recirculation system
  • fuel vapour recovery system.
  • engine cooling system.

The optimum operation of these systems is guaranteed by an electronic control system operated by a control unit.

Including the control unit operating logics makes it possible to manage the overall operation of the engine ASSEMBLY 10 system
