Applies to: all engines; all production periods

the lumbar adjustment takes place by pressing a special control at the side of the front seats.The control supplies a pump that fills the plastic bags located in the backrest with air.There is a protective thermal switch, known as a polyswitch, on the control power supply.

TEST RESULTS

ALL OK

ELECTRICAL SYSTEM CHECK.

ALL OK
