Applies to: all engines; all production periods

There are 2 additional current sockets, one of which is fitted as standard in the luggage compartment and one of which is avaiable as an option in the rear seat armrest.The two current sockets are not connected to each other, the rear current socket is connected by its power supply to the front current socket located in the dashboard, the socket in the armrest is connected by its power supply to the luggage compartment junction unit.
