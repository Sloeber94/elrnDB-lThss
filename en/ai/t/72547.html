Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The headlamp washers are activated at the same time as the windscreen washer and only with the dipped/main beam headlamps on;

the Body Computer node acquires both the windscreen washer/headlamp washer activation signal and the exterior lights control signal from the steering column switch unit and activates the headlamp washer pump via the relay.
