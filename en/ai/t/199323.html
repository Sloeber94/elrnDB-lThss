Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The radio reception and reproduction system has been developed taking the acoustic properties of the passenger compartment into account in order to offer optimum listening conditions.

The system is directly installed on the vehicle during production without subsequent operations and all the cables are integrated in the vehicle wiring.

The sytsem comes in two versions, a BASIC version and a HIFI (BOSE) version
