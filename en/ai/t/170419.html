Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 10V; all production periods; only with equipment: Passive Entry

On approaching the vehicle and pressing the electronic button on the handle the door does not open.

the central locking can be set by the user through the Connect to give the possibility of:the central locking can be set by the user through the Connect to give the possibility of:simultaneous opening of all the doors.opening the driver's door only.
