J02 WINDSCREEN WIPERS NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the ignition is on and the windscreen wiper control is operated, the windscreen wipers do not work;

the windscreen wiper and washing functions are managed by a control unit that receives commands from the right stalk lever.
