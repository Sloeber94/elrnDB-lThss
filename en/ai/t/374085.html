Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The adjustment is carried out by means of the joystick located at the side of the side and connected to the Driving Position Node.The Driving Position Node is, in turn, connected to the adjustment motor, the potentiometer connected to the motor memorizes the position of the seat.When the speed of the vehicle exceeds 10 Km/h the adjustment of the driver's seat by recalling the position memorized is not possible.
