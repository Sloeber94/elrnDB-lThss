1012B40 Counter-balance shaft crankcase bearings - Replace with engine and gearbox removed

Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the circlips (1a) and (1b) fastening the counter-balance shaft front bearing.

2. Remove the counter-balance shaft (2a) complete with rear bearing (2b) using a suitable drift.

3. Keep the rear plug.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 20V; all production periods

1. Remove the counter-balance shaft front bearing (1a) from the cylinder block/crankcase using tools (1b) and (1c).
