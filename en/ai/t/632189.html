Applies to: engines: 2.0 TB; all production periods

STRUCTURE

The unit is a system made up of the engine and all circuits required for its operation:

  • fuel feed circuit
  • air intake circuit
  • exhaust system with catalytic converter and lambda sensors
  • oil vapour recirculation system
  • fuel vapour recovery system.
  • engine cooling system.

The operation of these systems is optimised by an electronic control system governed by a control unit.

An understanding of control unit operating strategies gives us an overview of the system as a whole.
